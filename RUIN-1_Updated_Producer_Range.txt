RUIN-1 · UPDATED PRODUCER RANGE
================================
What the presets have to spread across so every type is covered, for the
current categories: Bass (long + short), Lead, Pad (incl. strings + brass),
Keys, Pluck, Arp / Sequence, Noise.

Part 1 = the axes every category spreads across.
Part 2 = per category: the roles and the types (sound families) that must
         each exist at least once.
Out of scope on purpose (decided outside the synth): width, reverb/delay
amount, EQ, rhythm/gates/pumping. LORN treatment (dark top, wear, slow life)
goes on everything; it is not an axis.


==============================================================================
PART 1 · THE AXES (every category, spread across each)
==============================================================================

1. ROLE        what the sound does in a track:
               bed · support · hook (melody) · rhythm (pattern) · accent
               (hits, stabs) · texture · transition (rise, fall, impact)
2. ATTACK      swell · soft (felt) · medium · pluck · snap
3. GRIT        clean · warm · dirty · destroyed
               (2 + 3 together = softness: about 1/3 soft, 1/3 middle, 1/3 hard)
4. LENGTH      blip · short · medium · long · ringing / endless
5. THICKNESS   thin · medium · thick · wall
6. REGISTER    sub · low · low-mid · mid · high · very high
7. PITCH       steady · drifting · wobbly / seasick · gliding · falling /
               rising · unpitched
8. MOVEMENT    still · slow life · evolving · restless
9. TOUCH       fixed (mechanical) · some · very expressive (velocity + wheel)
10. TONE COLOUR
               pure (sine) · round (triangle) · rich (saw) · hollow (square,
               pulse) · nasal / reedy · vocal · metallic (inharmonic) ·
               glassy (bell, FM) · woody · breathy · noisy · crushed / digital
11. MOOD       tender · nostalgic · melancholic · eerie · tense · menacing ·
               brutal · euphoric
12. ERA / LINEAGE
               70s analog · 80s poly / digital · 90s rave / digital ·
               modern hybrid · lo-fi / tape · industrial / machine
13. VOICING    mono · poly chords · both

PART 2 · PER CATEGORY
==============================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BASS  (long and short; both halves spread across the axes on their own)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Roles: foundation sub · moving mid-bass · bass line (melodic) · rhythmic
pattern · stab / hit · drone · transition (drop, slide).

Types, LONG:
- pure sub · warm sub (harmonic for small speakers) · infra rumble (felt only)
- Reese (two-saw beating) · wide unison Reese · neuro / RUIN Reese
- analog mono (Moog-style fat saw) · round triangle bass · hollow square bass
- PWM / Juno bass · supersaw bass · hoover bass
- distorted / fuzz / industrial saw · wavefolded · metallic FM-style · comb
- fifth / tritone / octave-screamer templates · deep two-octave rumble
- vocal / wavetable-morph bass
- drone bass (evolving, minutes long) · swelling / blooming bass · tape-worn
- breathing bass (rises after each hit) · organ bass · bowed / contrabass
- pedal bass (Taurus) · gliding / portamento line bass (big dives)

Types, SHORT:
- pluck bass · acid 303 · rubber / funk envelope · dub / sound-system
- 808 (clean, distorted, sliding) · kick-bass / sub thump · sub stab
- staccato square · octave gallop (synthwave) · off-beat (techno) · EBM 16ths
- FM slap / metallic plunk (Aprox) · log drum · donk · chip bass
- distorted stab · bit-crushed stab · sync-tear stab · Reese stab
- low-pass-gate / West Coast bongo · plucked string / upright
- tom-like / heartbeat bass · noise-burst bass · metal / anvil hit
- glitch blip · rumble (techno, reverb-fed)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEAD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Roles: main melody · counter-line · hook riff · solo · call / FX line.

Types:
- mono saw (Minimoog) · portamento saw · supersaw / anthem · octave-stacked saw
- hollow square / pulse · PWM soft lead · plucky square (Popcorn) ·
  staccato hook (Axel F)
- hard-sync tear lead · chainsaw · screaming distorted saw · fuzz guitar-like
- brass lead (CS-80, expressive) · distorted anthem brass (LORN Acid Rain)
- crying / beating detuned lead · eerie gliding lead · tape-warped lead
- sine whistle · theremin · Ondes · G-funk whine
- flute / breathy · reed / nasal · sax-shadow
- FM bell / glass lead · metallic / inharmonic lead
- acid 303 lead · resonant squeal / feedback lead
- vocal-ish / robot lead (Aprox) · noise lead (pitched noise)
- chip / lo-fi lead · broken-radio / band-limited lead · glitch lead
- space squiggle · laser / zap · phased whistle (Oxygène)
- shadow / low lead (under a vocal)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PAD  (incl. strings and brass)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Roles: bed · harmony · swell / build · stab · tension · texture.

Types, SYNTH PADS:
- warm analog poly · PWM chorus pad · supersaw wall · Juno / 80s pop pad
- dark bloom · drone pad (almost still) · evolving wavetable
- glassy / FM pad · shimmer · phaser / flanger (jet) pad
- tape-worn / seasick pad · dust / crackle pad · noise bed
- dissonant cluster · tritone / semitone rub · sour detuned
- breathy / air pad · choir-like "aah" (Aprox) · instant pad (no swell)
- reverse swell · rising-from-muffled · glitch / broken pad
- metallic / comb pad · distorted / burnt pad · rain / digital shimmer

Types, STRINGS:
- string machine (Solina) · ensemble · bowed slow · dark low cellos
- solo violin · high thin violins · sul ponticello (scratchy)
- tremolo / nervous strings · string stab (hammer section)
- staccato / spiccato ostinato · pizzicato (lives in Pluck)
- distant / far strings · aggressive / grinding strings

Types, BRASS:
- 80s poly brass stab · synth brass swell · soft / distant brass
- CS-80 brass (Blade Runner) · braam / monolith · warhorn
- low tubas / threat brass · French horn (soft, round)
- muted / thin trumpet · narrow biting trumpets
- sforzando horn (bite then sing) · falling / sagging brass
- fanfare · single signal horn (scooping)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEYS  (and key-like instruments)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Roles: chords · comping · motif / riff · stab · bed · solo.

Types:
- Rhodes / tine EP · phaser Rhodes · Wurlitzer · FM EP (DX7, Aprox)
- piano-like: felt · upright · honky-tonk · lo-fi grand · haunted detuned ·
  sustain-pedal wash · 80s piano + bell layer (all Aprox: synth versions)
- organs: drawbar · pipe / church · combo (Vox) · gothic / distorted ·
  soft sine organ · house organ stab · organ drone
- reeds: harmonium / pump organ · accordion / bandoneon · melodica
- bells / mallets: vibraphone · celesta · music box · toy piano · glass keys
- clav · harpsichord
- synth keys: chord stab · Prophet poly keys · cold darkwave keys ·
  80s/90s digital keys (bell over pad) · old-game / chip keys
- odd: reversed keys · ticking low-pass-gate keys · fluttering FM keys

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLUCK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Roles: motif · rhythm part · accent · texture tick.

Types:
- 80s analog filter pluck · trance supersaw pluck · thick detuned pluck
- house / dub chord stab · techno chord stab
- marimba · kalimba · steel drum · woodblock / bone knock
- harp · koto · pizzicato · muted guitar · palm-muted string · ringing wire
- bell pluck · glass pluck · uncanny inharmonic bell · metallic / industrial
- acid pluck · bubble · chip / glitch blip · UI blip
- lo-fi crushed pluck · tape bell · dark submerged mallet
- noise tick · tom tick · heartbeat pluck
- echo-made pluck (the pluck written for an echo after the synth)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ARP / SEQUENCE  (sounds made to be sequenced; the pattern comes from the DAW)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Roles: driving pulse · melodic arp · texture sequence · hypnotic loop.

Types:
- Moroder octave sequence (I Feel Love) · cold square sequence (Blue Monday)
- synthwave arp · darksynth relentless sequence · EBM 16th sequence
- acid / squelch sequence · random S&H bleeps
- warm analog arp with a slowly opening filter · soft sine / cradle arp
- music-box arp · piano-like FM arp (Children) · organ arp (Baba O'Riley)
- glassy FM sequence · metallic comb "machinery" · low-pass-gate twitch arp
- hot PWM sequence · supersaw / euphoric arp

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOISE  (the noise strip is the instrument: no oscillators, no sub)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Roles: bed · texture · hit / kit · playable line · transition.

Types (spread across all 14 noise types: 7 beds + their hits):
- beds: crackle · dust · static · tape hiss · air · rumble · white
- slowed / dark: hiss slowed into a roar · rumble an octave down · thumping
  crackle (OCT down)
- playable (KEY): noise lead · air glide · tape-stop line · static line
- kits (hits across the keys): crackle kit · static kit · dust kit · noise
  snare · breath puffs
- through effects: filtered noise sweep · crushed static · distorted rumble ·
  chorused air · RUIN-mangled noise
- transitions: riser (noise + rising filter) · downlifter / fall · tape stop ·
  noise impact


==============================================================================
HOW TO USE IT
==============================================================================
1. Every category: each type in Part 2 at least once.
2. Across a category: the axes in Part 1 spread out (no clustering on
   "dark + dirty + saw + long").
3. A batch is planned against the empty cells, not from a source list.
