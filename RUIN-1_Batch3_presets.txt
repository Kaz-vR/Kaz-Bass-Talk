RUIN-1 · BATCH 3 · PRESET RECIPES · STYLE CUM
==============================================
Style CUM = dark electronica / LORN, cinematic dystopian score, half-time
noir, haunted instrumental hip-hop, deep dubstep, witch house, industrial,
techno, dark ambient, synthwave, cyberpunk, apocalyptic, darksynth,
electro-industrial, EBM, darkwave, aggrotech, dystopian ambient, glitch,
noise. Built from RUIN-1_StyleCUM_sounds.txt with the synth's own controls
(OSC page, LFO page, FX page, RUIN page). Values are general sound-design
starting points, not tuned to this synth: dial by ear from there.

NAMING
- Category.Batch3.GenreSoundName, e.g. BassShort.Batch3.SynthwaveOutrunGallop.
- "Aprox" after the genre = the original needs something RUIN-1 doesn't
  have (FM, a formant filter, a real instrument ...); the Aprox line says
  what is missing and what stands in for it.
- Sounds that overlap Batch 1 / 2 are kept as Style CUM variants (a
  different take: EBM, darksynth, cyberpunk ...).

HOW TO READ THE RECIPES
- Voice: Mono (with legato / glide) or Poly; Analog = the analog drift amount.
- OSC A / OSC B: wave, OCT / SEM / FIN, UNI (unison voices) + Detune, Shape,
  Level, Lo cut. OSC B at level 0 = off.
- Sub: wave, OCT (relative to the note), level. Direct out ON unless noted
  (the sub then skips filter, dirt and effects: a clean low end).
- Noise: type, level, tone, tail, Lo cut. "hit" types = one burst per note.
- Filter: type, cutoff, res (resonance), Drive (the pre-filter drive in the
  Filter box), KEY (key tracking amount).
- Filter env: on/off, ENV = the filter envelope amount in octaves (the lit
  second ring on the Cutoff dial: how far the envelope sweeps the cutoff, + up
  or - down), and the filter envelope's A/D/S/R. "off (ENV 0)" = the filter
  envelope isn't used and the cutoff stays put (movement comes from LFOs).
- Amp: the amp envelope A/D/S/R.
- LFO / Mod: "LFO n shape rate → destination amount". Amounts are the matrix
  percentage. Scale: Fil Cutoff 10% ≈ 0.6 octave; A+B Pitch 1% ≈ 24 cents;
  Pitch 50% = 12 semitones. Drop LFO with a + amount = starts above the note
  and falls to it; bloom with a - amount = sinks below the note.
  Destinations are from the COMMON column unless a sound needs another.
- FX: in page order (Distortion, Crush, Tape, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, OTT,
  EQ). Anything not listed is off. No reverb / delay in the synth: add space
  after it ("Space after:" says what the sound wants).
- RUIN: off unless noted.
- Wheel: the mod wheel's move.
- Rhythm: sequences, gallops, off-beats and pumping are played or programmed
  in the DAW (and ducked with a volume shaper there); the recipe is the tone.
- Low-end rule for every bass: sub on Direct out, oscillators' Lo cut
  120-150 Hz, distortion "clean below" 120-200 Hz.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITS TO KEEP IN MIND FOR ALL OF THE ABOVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No arpeggiator, sequencer or gate: gallops, off-beats, 1/16 sequences and
  pumping are played or programmed in the DAW (volume shaper for pumping).
- No reverb or delay in the synth: rumbles, witch-house clouds, synthwave
  delays and dub echoes are added after it ("Space after" lines).
- "Aprox" presets get close but are not the real thing: no FM (DX7 keys and
  slap bass), no formant filter / vocoder (talking basses, robot leads,
  choirs), no real pianos.
- The rumble basses (TechnoRumbleDrone, TechnoRumbleKickBass) put distorted low end through the
  effects on purpose; check them in mono after adding reverb.
- One noise type at a time; the noise layer is shared by all voices. In
  chords only the lowest note plays the sub.
- No aftertouch or ribbon: CS-80 style pressure is played with velocity and
  the mod wheel (MPE in the plugin version).
