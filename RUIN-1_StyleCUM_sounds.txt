STYLE CUM · SOUND LIST (groundwork for Batch 3)
================================================
Style CUM = dark electronica / LORN, cinematic dystopian score, half-time
noir, haunted instrumental hip-hop, deep dubstep, witch house, industrial,
techno, dark ambient, synthwave, cyberpunk, apocalyptic, darksynth,
electro-industrial, EBM, darkwave, aggrotech, dystopian ambient, glitch,
noise.

Each sound: what it is, what it sounds like, where it lives (genres).
Descriptions only; recipes come with Batch 3.


==============================================================================
1. BASS LONG
==============================================================================

- Witch House Crushed Sub
  A pitched-down, crushed sub that sits under everything like a weight on
  the chest: a sine pushed into lo-fi saturation and bit-reduction, slow
  release. (witch house, haunted hip-hop)

- Detuned Witch Drone Bass
  Two saws far out of tune, beating slowly and seasick, low-passed dark,
  drenched in reverb above a dry sub. (witch house, darkwave)

- Darksynth Growl Saw
  A thick, mean, overdriven mono saw bass with a resonant low-pass that
  snarls when it opens: the Perturbator / Carpenter Brut "evil" low end.
  (darksynth, cyberpunk)

- Synthwave Octave Rolling Bass (held version)
  The classic 80s saw/pulse bass played as long notes: warm, chorused,
  analog, a bit brighter than the dark side. (synthwave, darksynth)

- Cyberpunk Neon Reese
  A wide, detuned Reese with a metallic, digital edge and a phaser
  moving through it: the city-at-night bass. (cyberpunk, darksynth, dark DnB)

- Apocalyptic Engine Drone
  A huge, slowly grinding low drone like a dying machine: saw plus
  rumble noise, heavy drive, the filter breathing over minutes.
  (apocalyptic, industrial, dark ambient)

- Dystopian Sine Pressure
  An almost pure sub drone that is felt more than heard, with a faint
  harmonic swelling and fading on top. (dystopian ambient, cinematic score)

- Industrial Distorted Mono
  A single-note distorted bass that never resolves: fuzz and hard clipping
  before a closed filter, clean sub beneath. (industrial, NIN-style, EBM)

- Darkwave Chorus Bass
  A post-punk/darkwave synth bass with heavy chorus: hollow pulse, mid
  focus, melancholic, the "cold wave" sound. (darkwave, coldwave)

- Techno Rumble Bass
  The long, dark "rumble" under industrial techno kicks: a low, distorted,
  reverb-smeared tone that fills the space between kicks. (industrial
  techno, dark techno)

- Hardwave Supersaw Bass
  A wide supersaw bass with distortion and a dark filter: big, cinematic,
  aggressive but smooth. (darksynth, hardwave, witch house crossover)

- Dubstep Sine Wobble (slow)
  A deep dubstep sine/square bass moving slowly, a lazy LFO on the filter,
  lots of air around it. (deep dubstep, post-dubstep)

- Glitch Crushed Drone
  A low drone run through bit-crushing and sample-rate reduction so it
  stutters and fizzes as it sustains. (glitch, noise)

- Noise Wall Bass
  A bass note buried in harsh distortion and static: more texture than
  tone, the note just readable underneath. (noise, power electronics, aggrotech)

- Cinematic Braam Bass
  The low "braaam" of dystopian trailers: brassy saws with a heavy filter
  swell and distortion. (cinematic score, apocalyptic)

- Ominous Fifth Drone
  A root and a dark fifth held together, tape-saturated, slowly
  moving: menacing and stable at once. (dark ambient, cinematic, LORN)

- Vocal-Formant Bass
  A low bass with a talking, vowel-like quality, "oh" to "ah" as the
  filter moves. (cyberpunk, neuro, dark electronica)

- Tape-Worn Mid Bass
  A warm, wobbly, half-broken mid bass, like it was recorded to an old
  cassette: wow, flutter, dull top. (LORN, haunted hip-hop, witch house)


==============================================================================
2. BASS SHORT
==============================================================================

- EBM Sequenced Bass
  The backbone of EBM: a tight, punchy saw/square bass playing relentless
  1/8 or 1/16 sequences, fast filter envelope, mechanical. (EBM, electro-industrial)

- Aggrotech Distorted Stab Bass
  A short, heavily distorted, aggressive bass hit with a bright filter
  snap, built to hammer with the kick. (aggrotech, hellektro)

- Rolling Off-Beat Bass
  Short bass notes rolling between kick hits (the "galloping" synthwave /
  techno bass), plucky and tight. (synthwave, techno, darksynth)

- Industrial Techno Thud
  A short, clipped, distorted low hit that doubles the kick: a dark
  transient with a gritty tail. (industrial techno)

- Acid Dark Line
  A 303 squelch kept dark and dirty: resonance, accent and distortion,
  used in a dark techno or electro context. (techno, electro, EBM)

- Electro Bass Hit
  A Detroit/Drexciya electro bass note: square or sine with a quick
  pitch drop, punchy, robotic. (electro, dark electronica)

- Witch House 808
  A slow, crushed, pitched-down 808 with a long distorted tail. (witch house, phonk)

- Distorted 808 Drop
  A short 808 with a big pitch fall and hard clipping. (haunted hip-hop, dark trap)

- Metallic FM Bass Stab
  A short bass with inharmonic, clangy partials, cold and machine-like.
  (industrial, EBM, glitch)

- Glitch Bass Blip
  A tiny, crushed, sample-rate-reduced bass blip, chopped and stuttering.
  (glitch, IDM)

- Darkwave Picked Bass
  A short, picked synth bass imitating a post-punk bass guitar: clicky
  attack, hollow body, chorus. (darkwave, coldwave)

- Cyberpunk Pluck Bass
  A short, bright, resonant digital bass pluck with a hint of sync.
  (cyberpunk, darksynth)

- Noise Burst Bass
  A sub hit layered with a burst of filtered noise: a door slam in the
  low end. (industrial, noise, apocalyptic)

- Dubstep Sub Stab
  A short sub hit with space around it, the deep dubstep "dub" note.
  (deep dubstep, post-dubstep)

- Hardwave Punch Bass
  A short, wide, distorted supersaw bass hit for drops. (hardwave, darksynth)

- Anvil Hit Bass
  A heavy, metallic, resonant hit, like iron on iron, with a sub. (LORN,
  industrial)

--- Techno short basses ---

- Rumble Kick-Bass
  The industrial-techno rumble: a short, distorted, reverb-smeared low tone
  played on the off-steps right after each kick, so kick and bass become one
  rolling mass. (industrial techno, dark techno)

- Hypnotic Techno Pluck Bass
  A short, dark, slightly resonant pluck bass playing a one-note or
  two-note loop for minutes, its filter moving very slowly so it hypnotises.
  (techno, minimal, dark techno)

- Peak-Time Distorted Bass
  A punchy, saturated saw bass on the off-beat, hard-clipped, made to
  hammer on a big system. (peak-time techno, industrial techno)

- Off-Beat Techno Bass
  A short sub/saw note on every off-beat 1/8 between the kicks: the classic
  "boom-dum" of driving techno. (techno, EBM-techno)

- Dub Techno Bass
  A deep, round, short sub note with a soft attack, swimming in delay above
  the sub. (dub techno, deep techno)

- Detroit Techno Square Bass
  A warm, short square bass with a quick filter snap: soulful, machine-like.
  (Detroit techno, electro)

- Minimal Blip Bass
  A tiny, clicky, very short sine/pulse bass blip, tightly gated, with a
  little pitch flick. (minimal techno, glitch)

- EBM-Techno Gallop Bass
  A 1/16 sequenced bass where each note is accented differently: the
  "body music" drive inside a techno tempo. (EBM techno, industrial techno)

- Metallic Techno Stab Bass
  A short bass with a clangy, ring-mod-like top, cold and percussive.
  (industrial techno, hard techno)

- Hard Techno Distorted Bass Hit
  A very short, very distorted bass hit almost merged with a hard kick,
  fast and brutal. (hard techno, schranz-adjacent)

--- Synthwave short basses ---

- Outrun Galloping Octave Bass
  The defining synthwave bass: a short saw/pulse playing driving 1/16
  octaves (low-high-low-high), bright filter snap, chorus. (synthwave, outrun)

- Juno Pulse Bass
  A short, round Juno-style pulse bass with chorus: warm, bouncy, 80s.
  (synthwave, retrowave)

- Moog-Style Short Bass
  A fat, short saw bass with a low-pass "bwow" envelope: punchy and warm.
  (synthwave, darksynth)

- Italo / Hi-NRG Octave Bass
  A tight, bright octave bass in 1/8 notes, sequenced, disco-driving.
  (synthwave, italo disco, hi-NRG)

- Carpenter Ostinato Bass
  A short, repeating, menacing pulse bass in a horror-score pattern, dark
  filter, slight drift. (darksynth, horror synth, synthwave)

- DX7 FM Slap Bass
  The 80s FM bass: a percussive, plucky, "slap" digital bass with a bright
  attack and woody body. (synthwave, retrowave)

- Sidechain-Feel Pluck Bass
  A short bass whose envelope pumps like it ducks to the kick: swelling in
  and cut short. (synthwave, retrowave)

- Heartbeat Pulse Bass
  A short, thudding Pro-One pulse in a heartbeat rhythm. (synthwave, darksynth)

--- Cyberpunk / darksynth short basses ---

- Darksynth Off-Beat Saw Bass
  A distorted, resonant saw bass on the off-beats, the Perturbator /
  Carpenter Brut drive under a lead. (darksynth, cyberpunk)

- Neon FM Growl Stab
  A short, metallic, FM-like growl hit, digital and mean. (cyberpunk, midtempo)

- Midtempo Cyberpunk Bass
  A slow-tempo, heavy, distorted saw/square bass stab with a metallic top,
  the "midtempo" cyberpunk punch. (cyberpunk, midtempo bass)

- Sync-Tear Bass Stab
  A short hard-sync bass whose sync sweep tears on every hit. (cyberpunk, darksynth)

- Bit-Crushed Cyber Stab
  A short, glitchy, crushed digital bass hit, hard and lo-fi. (cyberpunk, glitch)

- Formant Chop Bass
  A short bass note with a talking, vowel-like chop. (cyberpunk, neuro-adjacent)

- Distorted Reese Stab
  A short burst of a dark, detuned Reese, clipped. (cyberpunk, dark DnB)

- Cold Hardware Sequence Bass
  A robotic, perfectly even sequenced bass, no velocity, cold square tone.
  (cyberpunk, EBM, electro-industrial)

- Glitch Digital Bass
  A short digital bass that stutters and jumps in pitch. (cyberpunk, glitch)

- Laser Drop Bass
  A short bass with a fast, zappy downward pitch "pew", used on purpose for
  the retro-sci-fi feel. (cyberpunk, synthwave, electro)


==============================================================================
3. LEAD
==============================================================================

- Darksynth Screaming Saw Lead
  A distorted, aggressive mono saw lead with portamento: the Perturbator
  / Carpenter Brut hero lead. (darksynth, cyberpunk)

- Synthwave Hero Lead
  A bright, chorused, slightly detuned saw/square lead with vibrato and
  delay: the 80s soundtrack melody. (synthwave)

- EBM Mono Lead
  A cold, short-sustain mono lead, tight and robotic, often playing a
  sequenced motif. (EBM, electro-industrial)

- Aggrotech Hoover Lead
  A harsh, detuned, hoover-like lead, distorted, the stomping hook of
  aggrotech. (aggrotech, hellektro)

- Industrial Feedback Squeal
  A resonant, screaming lead that sounds like amp feedback. (industrial, noise)

- Cyberpunk Sync Lead
  A hard-synced lead that sweeps and tears, digital and neon. (cyberpunk, darksynth)

- Darkwave Melancholy Lead
  A cold, chorused, slightly out-of-tune lead with slow vibrato. (darkwave)

- Witch House Ghost Lead
  A detuned, pitched, ghostly lead drowned in reverb, vocal-like.
  (witch house)

- Dystopian Whistle Sine
  A thin, lonely sine lead high above a dark bed. (dystopian ambient, cinematic)

- Glitch Lead
  A lead that stutters, bit-crushes and jumps in pitch at random.
  (glitch, IDM)

- Acid Lead
  A resonant, squelching, distorted 303 lead. (techno, acid, EBM)

- Crying Detuned Lead
  Two oscillators beating slowly, bends into notes, late vibrato. (LORN)

- Brassy Cinematic Lead
  A brass-like saw lead with a filter swell, the CS-80 / Blade Runner
  lead. (cinematic, cyberpunk, synthwave)

- Noise Lead
  A pitched band of noise that sings a melody. (noise, dark ambient)

- Chiptune-Dark Lead
  A square lead with a lo-fi, crushed edge, old-game nostalgia turned
  sinister. (darksynth, glitch, cyberpunk)

- Formant Robot Lead
  A talking, vowel-shifting, robotic lead. (cyberpunk, electro)

- Supersaw Anthem Lead
  A huge detuned supersaw lead with distortion. (hardwave, darksynth)

4. PADS (including string and brass pads)
==============================================================================

- Synthwave Warm Poly Pad
  A lush, chorused Juno/Jupiter poly pad, nostalgic and warm. (synthwave)

- Darksynth Evil Pad
  A dark, detuned, slightly distorted pad, menacing and wide. (darksynth)

- Cyberpunk Rain Pad
  A cold, glassy, phased pad with digital shimmer, like neon reflected
  in rain. (cyberpunk, dystopian ambient)

- Blade Runner Brass Pad
  A CS-80 brass swell, slow bloom, majestic and sad. (cinematic, cyberpunk)

- Apocalyptic Braam Pad
  A massive, distorted brass wall that rises slowly. (apocalyptic, cinematic)

- Dark Ambient Drone Pad
  An evolving drone with filtered noise, low harmonics, near-motionless.
  (dark ambient)

- Witch House Reverb Cloud
  A detuned pad drowned in reverb and pitched down, eerie and slow.
  (witch house)

- Darkwave String Machine
  A cold string-machine pad (Solina-like), chorused and thin.
  (darkwave, coldwave)

- Haunted String Pad
  A mournful, slow string pad with drift and dust. (LORN, cinematic)

- Industrial Metal Pad
  A pad made of metallic, ringing, inharmonic tones and noise. (industrial)

- Glitch Pad
  A pad that crackles, stutters and breaks up as it sustains. (glitch)

- Noise Bed
  A pad of filtered hiss and static, pure texture. (noise, dark ambient)

- Dystopian Choir Pad
  A synthetic "aah" pad, cold and inhuman. (dystopian ambient, cinematic)

- EBM Stab Pad
  A short, punchy chord-pad for EBM/industrial stabs. (EBM, electro-industrial)

- Dissonant Cluster Pad
  Close, beating intervals, unresolved and tense. (dark ambient, horror, LORN)

- Tape-Worn Memory Pad
  A wobbly, worn pad like an old recording. (LORN, haunted hip-hop)

- Supersaw Wall Pad
  A huge detuned supersaw pad, dark and wide. (hardwave, darksynth)

- Low Brass Threat Pad
  Low, slow brass swells signalling danger. (cinematic, apocalyptic)

- Reverse Swell Pad
  A pad that swells in like a reversed tail. (dark ambient, cinematic)
