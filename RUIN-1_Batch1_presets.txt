RUIN-1 · BATCH 1 · PRESET RECIPES
==================================
Sound types RUIN-1 can make, per category, each with what it sounds like and
how to build it with the synth's own controls (OSC page, LFO page, FX page,
RUIN page). Values are starting points: dial by ear from there.

HOW TO READ THE RECIPES
- Voice: Mono (with legato / glide) or Poly; Analog = the analog drift amount.
- OSC A / OSC B: wave, OCT / SEM / FIN, UNI (unison voices) + Detune, Shape,
  Level, Lo cut. OSC B at level 0 = off.
- Sub: wave, OCT (relative to the note), level. Direct out ON unless noted
  (the sub then skips filter, dirt and effects: a clean low end).
- Noise: type, level, tone, tail, Lo cut. "hit" types = one burst per note.
- Filter: type, cutoff, res (resonance), Drive (the pre-filter drive in the
  Filter box), KEY (key tracking amount).
- Filter env: on/off, ENV = the filter envelope amount in octaves (the lit
  second ring on the Cutoff dial: how far the envelope sweeps the cutoff, + up
  or - down), and the filter envelope's A/D/S/R. "off (ENV 0)" = the filter
  envelope isn't used and the cutoff stays put (movement comes from LFOs).
- Amp: the amp envelope A/D/S/R.
- LFO / Mod: "LFO n shape rate → destination amount". Amounts are the matrix
  percentage. Scale: Fil Cutoff 10% ≈ 0.6 octave; A+B Pitch 1% ≈ 24 cents;
  Pitch 50% = 12 semitones down/up.
- FX: in page order (Distortion, Crush, Tape, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, OTT,
  EQ). Anything not listed is off. No reverb / delay in the synth: add space
  after it.
- RUIN: off unless noted.
- Wheel: the mod wheel's move.
- Low-end rule for every bass: sub on Direct out, oscillators' Lo cut
  120-150 Hz, distortion "clean below" 120-200 Hz.

BassLong.Batch1.Reese
  Sound: The classic dark drum & bass / LORN bass: two saws slightly out of
         tune, a slow beating "chorus" that moves by itself, warm and thick.
  Voice: Mono, legato, glide 40 ms, Analog 30%
  OSC A: saw, OCT 0, UNI 1, level 80%, Lo cut 130 Hz
  OSC B: saw, OCT 0, FIN -12 ct, level 70%, Lo cut 130 Hz
  Sub:   sine, OCT -1, level 60%
  Noise: dust, level 15%, tone 30%, tail 300 ms, Lo cut 400 Hz
  Filter: low-pass 24, cutoff 450 Hz, res 20%, Drive 35%, KEY 50%
  Filter env: on, ENV +1.5 oct (the ring on Cutoff), A 5 / D 700 / S 25% / R 200 ms
  Amp:   A 6 / D 300 / S 100% / R 200 ms
  LFO / Mod: LFO 1 slow drift 0.25 Hz → Fil Cutoff 8%;
             LFO 2 drift 1 Hz → A+B Pitch 0.4%; Velocity → Fil Cutoff 15%
  FX:    Distortion tube, drive 30%, mix 60%, clean below 150 Hz;
         Tape drive 30%, wow 15%, age 40%; Chorus ensemble mix 20%, Lo cut 250 Hz
  Wheel: snarl

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITS TO KEEP IN MIND FOR ALL OF THE ABOVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Acoustic instruments (piano, guitar, real strings, brass, mallets, bells,
  organs) are SYNTH versions: recognisable in character, not realistic.
- No reverb or delay in the synth: pads, plucks and dub chords need space
  added after it. No arpeggiator: arps are played or sequenced in the DAW.
- No rhythmic gating inside the synth (tremolo with an LFO on Level is
  possible; rhythmic chopping is done after the synth).
- One noise type at a time; the noise layer is shared by all voices.
- One main filter per voice; no formant/vowel filter.
- B Pitch / A Pitch alone (for sync sweeps) exist but are outside the COMMON
  destination column.
