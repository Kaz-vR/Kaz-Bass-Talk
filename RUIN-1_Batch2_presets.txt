RUIN-1 · BATCH 2 · PRESET RECIPES
==================================
Sounds taken from the artist reference (30 artists, six roles each), built
with RUIN-1's own controls (OSC page, LFO page, FX page, RUIN page). Values
are general sound-design starting points, not tuned to this synth: dial by
ear from there.

NAMING
- Category.Batch2.ArtistSoundName, e.g. BassLong.Batch2.LornBruisedMidBass.
- "Aprox" after the artist = the original sound needs something RUIN-1
  doesn't have (a real instrument, FM, a recording, a theremin, aftertouch,
  a vocoder ...); the recipe gets as close as it can, and the Aprox line says
  what is missing.
- Skipped: sounds that are clearly impossible here (real pianos, real
  strings and brass, voices, choirs, granular stretching, field recordings as
  the main sound), and sounds Batch 1 already covers.
- Categories: the six of Batch 1 plus 7. OTHER (drones, risers, impacts,
  sirens, rumbles, beds: everything outside the six).

HOW TO READ THE RECIPES
- Voice: Mono (with legato / glide) or Poly; Analog = the analog drift amount.
- OSC A / OSC B: wave, OCT / SEM / FIN, UNI (unison voices) + Detune, Shape,
  Level, Lo cut. OSC B at level 0 = off.
- Sub: wave, OCT (relative to the note), level. Direct out ON unless noted
  (the sub then skips filter, dirt and effects: a clean low end).
- Noise: type, level, tone, tail, Lo cut. "hit" types = one burst per note.
- Filter: type, cutoff, res (resonance), Drive (the pre-filter drive in the
  Filter box), KEY (key tracking amount).
- Filter env: on/off, ENV = the filter envelope amount in octaves (the lit
  second ring on the Cutoff dial: how far the envelope sweeps the cutoff, + up
  or - down), and the filter envelope's A/D/S/R. "off (ENV 0)" = the filter
  envelope isn't used and the cutoff stays put (movement comes from LFOs).
- Amp: the amp envelope A/D/S/R.
- LFO / Mod: "LFO n shape rate → destination amount". Amounts are the matrix
  percentage. Scale: Fil Cutoff 10% ≈ 0.6 octave; A+B Pitch 1% ≈ 24 cents;
  Pitch 50% = 12 semitones down/up.
- FX: in page order (Distortion, Crush, Tape, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, OTT,
  EQ). Anything not listed is off. No reverb / delay in the synth: add space
  after it ("Space after:" says what the sound wants).
- RUIN: off unless noted.
- Wheel: the mod wheel's move.
- Low-end rule for every bass: sub on Direct out, oscillators' Lo cut
  120-150 Hz, distortion "clean below" 120-200 Hz.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITS TO KEEP IN MIND FOR ALL OF THE ABOVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Aprox" presets get close but are not the real thing: no real pianos,
  guitars, brass, recorders, theremins, voices, vocoders, FM or recordings.
- Acoustic instruments in general (piano, strings, brass, mallets, organs)
  are SYNTH versions: recognisable in character, not realistic.
- No reverb or delay in the synth: most of these sounds want space added
  after it ("Space after" lines). No arpeggiator: arps are played or
  sequenced in the DAW.
- No rhythmic gating inside the synth (synced LFOs on Level or Cutoff give a
  pulse; tight pumping to a kick is done after the synth).
- No aftertouch or ribbon: CS-80 style pressure is played with velocity and
  the mod wheel (MPE in the plugin version).
- One noise type at a time; the noise layer is shared by all voices. In
  chords only the lowest note plays the sub.
- One main filter per voice; no formant/vowel filter.
- B Pitch / A Pitch alone (for sync sweeps) exist but are outside the COMMON
  destination column.
