# Hasselblad X2D camera UI

Group: **The Siren family** · Tags: `siren` `hardware` `minimal`
Source: https://www.hasselblad.com/x-system/x2d-100c/ (the maker's official product page; images are the maker's, kept here for study only)

**Why it is here:** A camera menu and touch screen with very few colours and big calm type: premium hardware UI

- **What makes it work:** The camera's back screen and top display: a few large numbers, plenty of black, almost no chrome.
- **Controls:** Physical dials and a touch screen with large tap targets.
- **Displays:** Big readable values (ISO, shutter) in white on black.
- **Colour:** Black and warm grey metal with one orange button.
- **Lesson for RUIN-1:** Large calm numbers on black read as premium; show the value, not a decoration.
