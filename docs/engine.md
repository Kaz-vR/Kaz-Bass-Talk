## Components

### 1. Synth engine
Up to 8 voices (`voice.polyphony`: 1 = monophonic with legato/glide, the bass default; 2-8 = polyphonic with voice stealing). Per voice:

- 2 main oscillators (sine, triangle, saw, pulse, wavetable with position (shown as MORPH), PWM-saw, physical-model string; and (owner's call, 27 Nov) **square** (a true square at Shape 0, every odd harmonic up to 1023 at exactly 1/k, built from its spectrum, `squareCycle`; Shape rounds it towards a sine), **double saw, sync saw, vowel, choir, talk, bell, ring, digital, glass, bowed, reed, breath, electric guitar** (owner's call, 27 Nov: the WORN waves, tape saw, cassette pulse, lofi sample and broken sine, removed from both engines and the menu), and (owner's call, 27 Nov) the real **wavetables**, made to be scanned with Shape (9 keyframes, 2 frames a step): **acid**, **scream**, **growl** (a neuro yoi), **fold**, **harmonic** (partials one by one), **sync sweep** (`sync saw`), **crush**, **spectral** (a moving comb), **formant scan**, **gritty**; (owner's call, 27 Nov: exactly Serum's) **acid, ring, scream and gritty are sampled**: Serum's own tables, frame by frame from the owner's renders (`tools/waves/*.wav`, 2048 samples a frame: acid 40 frames, ring 33, scream 123, gritty 4); and (owner's call, 27 Nov, after learning from them: made the way Serum's tables are, not as formulas) **every other bank wave but square, harmonic and four the owner kept as formulas (double saw, bell, digital, glass: his before/after picks, 27 Nov, `RUIN-1 Wave Picks`) is processed**: a real source through real processing, one cycle at 16x resolution then cut to 1023 harmonics, full band, 32-128 frames each a little different from its neighbours, the frames keeping their natural levels (`tools/build-waves.py`: glottal pulses through a parallel formant bank for vowel / choir / talk / formant scan and the MORPH table's vocal end, a phase-modulated saw through moving narrow formants driven hard for growl, Helmholtz bow motion and a wooden body for bowed, a soft-clipped reed for reed, a flute jet for breath, a plucked string, tube preamp, tone stack and cab for e-guitar, a sampler for crush, wavefolders for fold, continuous hard sync for sync sweep, a moving comb for spectral); both taken by `tools/extract-waves.py` (DC removed per frame, the whole table scaled by one number, int16 in `wave-tables.ts` and `generated/WaveTables.h`), one frame per Shape step, not peak-normalised per frame (`BANK_SAMPLED`): each a small morph table of the **wave bank** (`BANK` / `bankKey` in `wavetable.ts`, `Wavetable.h`: 3 or 5 keyframes as one cycle in time, FFT band-limited and mip-mapped like the morph table, built at start-up after it, ~1.7 s, ~106 MB of RAM, nothing on disk), Shape (`wt_pos`) moving through its frames; every one is only its table, nothing layered or added (owner's call: no hidden effects, so the supersaw and the breath's noise were taken out again: saw with UNI 7 is the supersaw, the Noise box's air bed the breath), each with detune, unison (1–7 voices), spread, level (unison as Serum's, measured 27 Nov, owner's call: the voices evenly over ±detune/2, detune up to 400 cents outer to outer (±2 semitones) on Serum's knob curve, knob^2.5; the voices on alternating sides from the outside in and mirrored, the middle one of an odd count in the centre, so every detune is heard on both sides; each copy at a new random phase on every note, voice 0 at 0 for a steady punch; level / √voices, so 7 voices are as loud as 1); no FM (owner's call: removed from both engines, its controls and its matrix destination; old patches' FM settings are ignored and the bank's FM keys, tines and bells play without it until the rebuild)
- Sub oscillator (sine, warm sine, triangle, square, pulse, saw; −4 to +4 octaves), always mono; noise. *(The click / attack transient layer was removed, owner's call. Fourteen types: seven beds and a hit of each, the menu in four columns, every hit in the same row as its bed (owner's call: no pitch and no rhythm anywhere; hum, machine and wind were tried and dropped, they load as rumble / air): GRAIN (crackle: vinyl pops only; dust; static: a steady grainy fizz with crackles, its flicker far too fast to hear as rhythm) · NOISE (tape hiss: steady, no flutter or dropouts; air; rumble; white noise: plain, straight up, the full range at Tone's top, Tone darkening it, Density its width) · HIT (crackle hit, dust hit, static hit) · HIT (tape hiss hit, air hit, rumble hit, white noise hit). A **hit** plays its bed's own generator inside one burst per new note, fading over Tail, nothing between, harder notes bursting bigger (`TEX_BED` in `src/engine/core/voicefx.ts`): a grain hit starts on its first grain (grains four times as dense), a noise hit swells in over ~10 ms. **Air hit is the breath** (shown as "air hit (breath)"): the puff on brass, bowed strings, flutes and keys; old presets' breath went where it leaned (`migrateBreath`: an attack chiff became air hit, Tail ~3× its decay; a sustained breath became the air bed). A **Density** dial (`texture.density`, 0.5 = each type's classic sound; the old Character is converted) on every type: on the grains how many (pops, ticks, crackles), on the noise how thick (how much of the range the band fills, its level held even). **Every type plays at the same loudness** wherever its Tone is: a trim table (`TEX_TRIM_DB`, K-weighted as LUFS, hits over their burst) made by `tools/level-texture.ts` and checked by `tests/texture.test.ts`; rerun the tool after changing a type's sound. An **OCT** box in the Noise title row (`texture.octave`, -6..+2: owner's pick after testing, -6 for experimental moments, above +2 changes little) plays the bed slower or faster like tape (the bed runs at 2^oct of the sample rate and is read back interpolated): down is darker and bigger (crackle becomes dark thumps, hiss sinks to rumble), up is smaller and quicker; the print stretches with it. A **KEY** lamp in the print's corner (`texture.key`) makes the noise **playable**: its speed follows the note like a sample across the keyboard (middle C at the OCT setting, an octave up twice as fast), with glide and pitch bend sliding it (glide from a high note to a low one for a tape stop; the HIT types become a noise kit across the keys), always within -6..+2; the bed is shared, so with chords it follows the newest note. The matrix's **Noise pitch** destination (`noise_pitch`, in the PITCH column of the destination menu, ±6 octaves at full amount, the newest voice's, as the FX targets) moves the speed on top: a drop LFO for a tape stop on every note, an LFO for a roar-to-fizz swing, velocity for a deeper dive. No combinations of types (owner's call).)*
- **GRIND** (`src/engine/core/grind.ts`, `grind.on` + `grind1..4.*`, off by default): switching it on starts from Destroyed Reese's chain (its values are the schema's GRIND defaults, owner's call; the other presets carry no GRIND values of their own until the rebuild); the neuro-bass chain, four stages **on the summed sound** (owner's calls: it sat before the main filter, where a dark preset's low-pass threw away most of what it made, and then inside each voice, where a chord could never grind against itself; now every voice (oscillators → pre-filter drive → main filter → amp) is summed, the noise bed added unless it is on Direct out or NOISE THRU, and the sum runs once through **RUIN → DRIVE → bitcrusher → tape** (`DirtBus`, `src/engine/core/bus.ts` / `plugin/engine/Bus.h`), RUIN and DRIVE 2x oversampled, always, so the latency never changes; the clean sub waits as long and joins after the effects; RUIN's KEY and ONE SHOT follow the newest note, the matrix's DRIVE / crush / tape targets the newest voice). **Mix** (`grind.mix`, dry / wet for the whole chain: the untouched input against the chain, OTT included, so Mix 0 is exactly the chain switched off), **Lo cut** (`grind.lowcut`: below it the sound passes clean, a 24 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley split), **KEY** (`grind.key`: every stage follows the note, Freq is where it sits at C3), **ONE SHOT** (`grind.oneshot`: stages moving with the note become an envelope: from the top of the range they fall fast and settle at the bottom until the next note, Rate shown as the sweep time in ms; the note movement restarts on every new note, legato included) and **OTT** (`grind.ott`, Depth: Ableton's OTT preset / Xfer's OTT, as extreme: three bands split at 88 Hz and 2.5 kHz, 1:66 down above and 4.17:1 up below, the thresholds, band gains and attack / release times fitted to Xfer's OTT (owner's call, 27 Nov, measured headless with `tools/ref-host` and `tools/ott-ref.ts`: tones stepped -60 … 0 dBFS through each band within ~1.5 dB, level steps within ~1 dB on average: attacks 12 / 5.6 / 3.4 ms, releases 70 / 70 / 33 ms, four times faster than Ableton's preset), the detector calibrated to the synth's bus level (+18 dB) with +15 dB makeup, so full Depth is louder and squashed with the tails pulled up; it sits on the whole synth after the amp envelopes, the voices and the noise bed before the effects, so it works on attacks and tails; the clean sub and everything under GRIND's Lo cut stay out; owner's call: the one exception to the no-compressor rule; neither OTT lifts anything below -80 dBFS (full lift from -70), so tails and residue never come back as hiss or ringing in silence). Nothing in the chain is level-matched (owner's call: push it hot): more Drive is louder and denser and hits the next stage harder, and RUIN's **Output** (`grind.output`, -24 … +12 dB, on the strip after Mix) sets the chain's level at the end. **No ONE SHOT** (owner's call, 24 Nov): both engines ignore `grind.oneshot` / `oneshot_dir` (kept in the schema only so old patches load), every stage always moves on its clock; the old filter types stay in the engine only for old patches, the bank and the page use BELLS alone. **PHASER parts** (owner's call, `phaser 1-8` in the boxes, `grind<n>.phaser_on/_level`, `ph_*`): up to 12 notches (`ph_notches`), each a second-order all-pass (SVF, Q 1.1) at CENTER ± SPREAD octaves apart, swept together AMOUNT octaves by the part's own LFO (`ph_clock`: HZ free running, SYNC on the bar, RANDOM wandering; `ph_rate`), the right channel STEREO degrees ahead (on RANDOM a blend towards its own path), FEEDBACK (0-0.9) around the all-passes, the dry + all-passes levelled as the old phaser filter; its live L / R LFO goes to the page packed (`packPhaser`) on the worklet's 4th output (8 channels; the plugin's live values 32-39). Each stage is a moving filter (notch, peak, band, comb, phaser, **bells**: owner's pick, formant was dropped; **bells** (owner's call) is the filter as an EQ: four bands in a row, each with its own **type** as EQ Eight's eight (`grind<n>.bell1..4_type`, owner's call: low cut 48 / 12 dB, low shelf, bell (default), notch, high shelf, high cut 12 / 48 dB; one SVF mixed c0·x + c1·bp + c2·lp + c3·hp, k = 1/Q, a bell x + (gain − 1)·k·bp, the shelves after Simper (the corner moved by √A so the gain's middle sits at Freq), the 48 dB cuts four Butterworth sections, the last one's Q times Q / 0.71; Gain works on the bell and the shelves; and the old filters as band types (owner's call): band (only the band round Freq, `k·bp`), comb (a delay of one period of Freq, Gain = feedback ±0.92, minus = hollow, its peaks at 0 dB) and phaser (four first-order all-passes at Freq with feedback 0.85 × log2(1+Q)/log2(19), mixed with the dry); `grind<n>.bell_count` 1-4 = how many bands are used), Freq, Gain (±18 dB), Q and range (`_freq/_gain/_q/_range`), each moving in its own zone (its Freq × KEY ± half its range, slid inside 20 Hz-20 kHz) on the box's Rate and clock, each on its own path (HZ / SYNC a quarter cycle apart, RANDOM each wandering on its own; ONE SHOT sweeps them together), each band in its own **direction** (owner's call, 26 Nov: `grind<n>.bell<k>_dir` left right = its frequency moves in its range, up down = its gain moves ± half its up down range `bell<k>_vrange` (0-36 dB) around its Gain, cross = both on the same path; moving up down only, band 1's live value carries its position as an octave around its Freq for the page), or with **LINK** (`grind<n>.bell_link`, owner's call) all on band 1's path, together (a vowel's formants); no Width or Depth; the page reads bells 2-4's positions packed into the stage's live resonance value (`packBells`); levelled like EQ Eight (owner's call): wherever a filter isn't working it sits at exactly 0 dB and only the peak rises above it: notch flat with its dip, peak a bell up to +17 dB by Depth on a flat 0 dB, band's centre at 0 dB, comb's teeth at 0 dB with the dips between, phaser 0 dB away from its notches and its feedback peaks held to 0 dB) into its own drive, each switched off on its own (`grind<n>.filter_on` / `drive_on`, each box's thin on/off bar: FILTER n and DRIVE n, the stage on while either is, owner's call: a stage can be only its drive or only its filter) (owner's call: **tape** (highs pushed into a soft knee first by pre-emphasis and taken back after, a little top lost), **tube** (the lopsided tanh, even harmonics, a soft top), **amp** (a tightened input into a hot asymmetric preamp, a 4x12 cab through a close mic: low cut, a bump at 110 Hz, boxy mids scooped, bite at 2.4 kHz, a steep top at 5.2 kHz, then a few darker early reflections of a small room) **soft clip** (cubic), and the hard ones for neuro's bite (owner's call, under review): **hard clip**, **wavefold** (a sine fold), **rectify** (full-wave, the octave up), **downsample** (sample-and-hold, Drive taking the rate down to 1/128, no ADAA: aliasing on purpose) and **diode** (soft on top, clipping early below), and **starved** (owner's call, the Culture Vulture's starving as a type rather than a Bias dial: a tube that conducts only past a threshold, a dead zone around the centre, so quiet parts sputter, crackle and gate and only the big swings get through, lopsided; more Drive lifts more past it), every type but downsample followed by a DC blocker so no box pushes the next off centre; the curves' antiderivatives (ADAA) are taken from 0, so the types joined at 0 (rectify, diode, starved) stay continuous (before that fix rectify and diode jumped at every zero crossing: spikes, +40 dB, all distortion); the FX page's DRIVE has the same nine; their menu (owner's pick) is four columns by what they do, gentle to destroyed: WARM (tape, tube, starved, amp) · CLIP (soft clip, hard clip, diode) · BREAK (wavefold, rectify) · DIGITAL (downsample), each entry with a tiny picture of one sine cycle through it (`driveTypeSelector`, `driveIconPath`; the dropdown draws an option's `data-icon`); the defaults stage 1-4 tape, tube, amp, soft clip, Destroyed Reese's too; old patches' soft / hard load as soft clip, fold / fuzz as amp; ADAA on every curve). Each drive is laid out as Ableton's Overdrive (owner's call): a **band** in front of it (`grind<n>.drive_freq` 50 Hz-18 kHz, `drive_width` 0.5-9 octaves, 9 = the whole sound; owner's call, like a multiband plugin rather than Overdrive: a 24 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley split into below / the band / above, only the band driven and blended by Dry/Wet, below and above passing untouched and added back, so driving the top never loses the bottom (it used to: the band's outside was thrown away at full Dry/Wet); the FX page's DRIVE the same, `Stage.split`), shown as a curve with a ball you drag (sideways = where, up / down = how wide, double-click = full), then the drive, then **Tone** (`drive_tone`, a low-pass 200 Hz-20 kHz after it, fully up = open, its dotted top on the print); no level matching (`drive_dyn` / `dist.dyn` stay only so old patches load); the stage's Mix is its **Dry/Wet**. The drive box's dials: Drive, Tone, Dry/Wet (owner's call: the Bias dial and the OTT after each drive were removed as complexity; Bias lives on as the starved type, and the page's level meters show when a stage hits the next too hard). Drive (0 … +30 dB, even across the knob) on whatever level arrives (a hotter stage before it means more dirt here); with Mix; the filter moves around Freq by ±Range/2 octaves, moved at Rate, the clock (`grind<n>.clock`) being the movement (owner's call: the old NOTE / WANDER move switch was removed, since wander on Hz or Sync sounded like Rand anyway): **Hz** (an even swing up and down at exactly Rate, restarting from the top at every note), **Sync** (the same swing on the bar, 1 bar … 1/32; the old beat movement and `sync` load as Sync) or **Rand** (wander: smooth random, never repeating, each step at its own speed, up to ~3× either way around Rate; old patches with wander on Hz or Sync load as Rand; rolls pick Rand seven times out of ten, else Hz, never Sync: life, not the grid). ONE SHOT (one sweep per note that holds at its end) works on all three clocks, on Rand each note's sweep at its own speed around Rate; its direction (`grind.oneshot_dir`, the arrow beside it, owner's call): down = from the top of the range to the bottom (right to left, the default), up = from the bottom to the top (left to right). Each filter has **Width** (`grind<n>.width`, owner's call: the old Res turned round, up = wider: a wider notch or band, a broader peak, softer comb teeth and phaser notches) and **Depth** (`grind<n>.depth`: how deep it goes, the filter blended in as 1 + Depth (H − 1): 0 = a straight line, no effect; the peak up to +17 dB at full Depth); only the frequency moves (owner's call: no vertical movement; V Range and the wandering resonance were removed, old patches load Res as Width and keep Depth full). Each stage's live frequency goes to the page on worklet control channels 12-15 and 24-27 (stages 5-8; their resonance 16-19 and 28-31; the worklet's control output has 32 channels, the plugin's `live` array 32 too) (`EngineHost.liveGrind`). A DC blocker ends the chain. About 3.5 % CPU per voice with all four stages (meant for mono basses; not optimized yet). In both engines (`plugin/engine/Grind.h`).
- **Oct down** (`voice.halfspeed`, a lamp on the play strip after Legato; owner's call): the whole synth at half speed, live, as a frozen render pitched down an octave in Ableton's Repitch mode (everything an octave lower and darker, envelopes, glides, LFOs and effects twice as slow, notes still starting when played): the engine renders one sample for every two played, read back through a windowed-sinc 2× interpolator, then the output's safety stages again at the real rate (subsonic / DC filter); it switches at the bottom of the engine's duck, held silent until the interpolator has emptied (no click); synced clocks free-run at half the host's tempo while it is on. Both engines.
- Pre-filter drive → filter (4-pole low-pass, band-pass, formant (vowels, for choir), 303 diode with accent, notch, comb, low-pass gate, 4-pole high-pass: sweep it up to thin a sound out; (owner's call, 27 Nov) **low-pass 12** (a clean 12 dB SVF), **MS-20** (a 12 dB low-pass driven hard in and out, resonance near screaming), **phaser** (four all-passes at Cutoff with the dry, Res feeding back), **ring** (times a sine at Cutoff Hz, Res how much), **decimate** (held at Cutoff as a sample rate, Res taking the bits from 16 to 3), **talk** (the formant filter with Cutoff sweeping the vowel, the throat fixed); and **SEM** (`sem`, owner's call: an Oberheim SEM-style 12 dB low-pass, a bending state-variable filter, softer and more open than the ladder, keeping its bass when resonant)) → **DRIVE** (owner's call: the FX page's distortion became RUIN's drive, `DriveUnit` in `grind.ts` / `Grind.h`, the same code a RUIN stage runs: `dist.type` tape / tube / amp / soft clip, the Overdrive band (`dist.band_freq`, `dist.band_width`) on a pad you drag, drawn like a RUIN drive (shaded band, a ring, Tone as a dotted line), Drive, Tone (`dist.post_tone`, fully up = open), Dry/Wet (`dist.mix`), **Output** (`dist.output`, -24 … +12 dB: it is pushed hot, not level-matched); old patches' soft loads as tape, hard as soft clip, fold / fuzz as amp; `dist.clean_below` keeps the part below it clean), on the summed voices (see GRIND above) → bitcrusher → tape; in each voice: amp → **mid clip** (transparent soft clipper on osc 1 + 2, 0–12 dB drive, 0 = off)

### VIBRATO (LFO shapes 13-17, owner's call: a realistic vibrato, played with the mod wheel)

Sine (13, the classic), triangle (14, from 0 like the sine), saw up (15), saw down (16) or square (17) (`vibWave`),
running per note (restarting with each note), never on without the wheel: its width is the mod wheel squared
(the first half gentle) times its own Depth (`lfo<n>.vib_depth`: 0 to ±50 cents on a pitch target, 0-100 % like an
LFO's Depth on any other (`isPitchDest`); the matrix slot's amount is ignored for a VIBRATO source), at its own Rate (`lfo<n>.vib_rate`, 3-12 Hz, or synced), which the
wheel speeds up slightly (x 0.9 at the bottom to x 1.1 at the top). No LFO fades in any more (`lfo<n>.fade` is unused,
kept so old presets load). Both engines (`voice.ts`, `Voice.h`).

The mod wheel plays only the VIBRATO shapes (owner's call): its eight connected moves were removed from `extraModulation` in both engines.

### The LFO page's filters (owner's call, 24 Nov)

Six matrix destinations of their own (`D_LF_ALL`..`D_LF_NOISE`, 47-52: `lfo_filter`, `lfo_filter_ab`, `lfo_filter_a`,
`lfo_filter_b`, `lfo_filter_sub`, `lfo_filter_noise`), apart from the OSC page's filter and its A / B / S / N lamps:
12 dB low-passes (`Svf12`, Simper's SVF at Q 0.707) on the whole voice after everything (the sub included, at its
output), on A, on B (A + B = both), on the sub and on the noise. Each is open (20 kHz) at the top of its routes' swing
and 8 octaves lower at the bottom: closed by `LF_OCTAVES / 2 x (swing - value)` octaves, the swing being the sum of its
routes' |amount| (a VIBRATO's times the wheel squared), so no route, Depth 0 or a vibrato at wheel 0 leaves the sound
untouched. RESONANCE stays the OSC page filter's. The vibrato's value is smoothed over ~3 ms so its square and saws
never click on a volume or filter target. LFO routes to the OSC page's cutoff were moved to FILTER
(`tools/convert-lfo-filters.ts`). Both engines.

The custom LFO's HZ mode (`wobble.free`, `wobble.hz`): its cycle is `tempo / 60 / hz` beats, so everything else (one shot, shift, random steps) runs as in SYNC. Both engines.

### RUIN drive targets and the custom LFO's targets (owner's call, 25 Nov)

`ruin_drives` (53) moves every RUIN drive, `ruin_drive1`..`8` (54-61) the drive in that RUIN box; each relative to its
own Drive (`drive x (1 + value)`, clamped 0-1), its gain glided (`gainT`, LV_SMOOTH) so it never steps; the bus hands
the newest voice's matrix to `Grind.control`. FX DRIVE is `dist_drive` (added to its Drive). The custom LFO has three
targets (`wobble.dest`, `dest2`, `dest3`) with signed depths (`wobble.amt1-3`, -1..1): FILTER (`lfo_filter`) = its own
filter, VOLUME (`amp`) = its own gate (minus = the shape upside down), the rest `v x amt` into every voice's matrix.
`wobble.depth`, `wobble.cutoff`, `wobble.volume` are unused now (old patches migrate in `migrateWobTargets`).

**Four custom LFOs** (owner's call, 25 Nov): `wobble.*` and `wobble2.*` .. `wobble4.*`, each its own shape, rate,
one shot, Smooth, shift and **four targets** (`dest`..`dest4`, `amt1-4`, `offset1-4`), run by `WobUnit`
(`src/engine/core/wobunit.ts`, `plugin/engine/WobUnit.h`), each with its own random source. Each target follows
**its own line**: `y = master x (depth x (v - 0.5) + offset / 2)`, so depth 100 % = the shape itself (a new
target's start), 0 = flat, -100 % = upside down, Offset (starting at 0) moves the line up or down by up to half the
range, and the **master Depth** (`wobble.master`, 0-1, default 1) scales all of them (0 = the LFO does nothing). A
matrix target moves by `y` around where it is set; the FILTER lines of all four multiply into their one own filter
(a 24 dB low-pass open at the top of the line, shut at 60 Hz at its bottom), the VOLUME lines into their one gate
(the level). **No Lo cut** (owner's call: it only made sense for Filter and Volume; `wobble.lowcut` removed from
both engines and the schema), so the gate and filter work on the whole sound. Their phases go to the page two to a
value (`packWob`, worklet control channels 2 and 11, the plugin's live values 2 and 3). Older patches are converted
to the same sound (`migrateWobOffsets`: matrix offset = depth x o, filter / gate offset = o x (1 - |depth|), o the
old `wobble.off` or +1). Both engines.

### PITCH SHIFTER (owner's call, 27 Nov: in the delay's place)

The FX page's delay is gone from both engines, the schema and the page (kept for recall in `archive/fx-delay.md`; its
matrix slots `delay_time` / `delay_mix` stay only so the other destinations keep their numbers). In its place, after
HYPER and before the reverb, the **PITCH SHIFTER** (`PitchShifter`, fx.ts / Fx.h; `shifter.*`): the reverb's octave-down
shifter generalised: two read taps sliding through a short line at the ratio's speed, crossfaded sin² / cos², so the
body moves with the pitch (the monstrous octave down). **Oct** (±3), **Sem** (±11), **Fine** (±100 ct) add up to one
shift; an anti-alias low-pass in front when it shifts up. **Grain**: the window from 80 ms (smooth) down to 6 ms, and
each grain's start jittering (grain² x half a window), so it goes granular and warbly. **Feedback** (x0.95): the shifted
sound back in through a soft limit and a 7 kHz darkening, so it keeps climbing (shimmer) or falling. **Formant** (±24
st, 0 = the body moves with the pitch): 24 log-spaced bands (60 Hz-15 kHz, Q 4.2) over the shifted sound, each band's
gain its own envelope read Formant semitones away (down = the same note from something far bigger), held at the level
it came in at and faded in over the first semitone. **Mix** crossfades the dry to the shifted above **Lo cut** (below
it stays dry). Every dial is an LFO target.

### The bangs (owner's bug, 27 Nov)

Two causes, both fixed in both engines. (1) A drive's antiderivative was reset to 0 when the chain was cleared and
started at 0, but for tape, transformer, console, triode and amp the curve's antiderivative at rest isn't 0, so the
first sample after a reset (a note from silence) came out at about +30 dBFS; it is now set to the curve's own value at 0,
and a stage starts with no type (-1) so its first control sets everything up. (2) Every OTT lifts quiet passages a lot
(up to its upward limit plus the band gain and makeup) and needs a few ms to pull that back when a loud edge follows, so
those few ms came out tens of dB too loud, compounding through the chain's OTTs (OTT OUT is 100 % by default); each OTT
band now has a soft ceiling 12 dB over what it puts out for a full-scale input (`c·tanh(x/c)`), which normal levels never
reach. The RUIN chain's worst peaks went from +30 / +41 dBFS to about -2 dBFS with the same average level.

### 27 Nov fixes (owner's calls)

- Custom LFOs: a row with no target no longer counts (with every target gone it does nothing). `wobble*.oneshot` is **RETRIGGER**: the cycle restarts from every note and keeps running (was: once, then holding); a legato note (mono, legato on, a key still held) doesn't restart it, as the other LFOs.

### Drive types (owner's call, 27 Nov: rebuilt; this replaces the type list in the GRIND paragraph above)

RUIN's drives and the FX page's DRIVE share 17 types (`grind<n>.drive_type`, `dist.type`), in five groups by where they work. Loading a type (menu, ‹ › stepper, or a new drive box) moves the drive band onto its group's range exactly (centre the geometric middle, width log2(hi / lo) octaves; FULL SPECTRUM = 9, the whole sound):

| Group | Range | Types (curve; colour) |
|---|---|---|
| SUB & BASS (weight & loudness) | 20–250 Hz | transformer saturation (lows +8 dB under 200 Hz into a lopsided algebraic curve, taken back after), soft clipper (cubic), hard clipper |
| LOW-MIDS (warmth & body) | 250–800 Hz | triode tube saturation (the old tube: lopsided tanh, 9 kHz loss), overdrive (x2, 150 Hz tight, negative side clips at 0.6, 5 kHz loss) |
| UPPER-MIDS (bite & cut) | 800 Hz–5 kHz | transistor saturation (x1.5, 200 Hz tight, clean to 0.7 then a hard knee), pentode tube saturation (steep tanh, 12 kHz loss), distortion (x4 tanh, 100 Hz tight, 7 kHz loss), amp simulation (the old amp: cab and room), wavefolder, rectifier |
| HIGHS & AIR (sparkle & texture) | 5–20 kHz | exciter (0.6 tanh + 0.4 |tanh|: even and odd harmonics on top), bitcrusher (16 bits down to 2 with Drive), downsampler (the old sample-and-hold) |
| FULL SPECTRUM (glue & cohesion) | 20 Hz–20 kHz | tape saturation (the old tape), console saturation (x0.5, a gentle algebraic curve), fuzz (x8, 60 Hz tight, negative side clips at 0.45, 8 kHz loss) |

Every curve has its exact antiderivative (ADAA) and a DC blocker after it. diode and starved are gone; old patches load diode as distortion, starved as fuzz, and the other old names as their new ones (`migrateDriveTypes`). The menu shows each group's title, range and purpose over its column, and an info box under the columns with the hovered type's "Name: what it does".

### OTT BOXES and OTT OUT (owner's call, 27 Nov)

RUIN has two OTTs, both Depth knobs on its strip next to the randomizer. **OTT BOXES** (`grind.ott_box`, default 0): every box in the chain (filter, drive, EQ, phaser) runs its own OTT right after its level, all set by this one knob; it is RUIN's OTT (the same `Ott` class, Xfer-fitted bands and times, no Lo cut split of its own: the chain only sees what is above RUIN's Lo cut), at the chain's oversampled rate, so the next box, the listening taps (solo / bypass / delta) and the meters hear it. **OTT DRIVES** (`grind.ott_drives`, default 0): the same, after the drive boxes only (a second OTT on each drive box, after OTT BOXES' when both are up). **OTT OUT** is `grind.ott`, the post OTT after the chain. Both are LFO targets.

### Pan targets (owner's call, 27 Nov)

Five fixed destinations after the RUIN drives (`D_PAN` 62 .. `D_PAN_SUB` 66, so `D_PARAM0` is 67): **pan** (the whole sound, sub and NOISE THRU included, after the sub joins, before the DC filters and width), **pan_ab** (adds to both oscillators), **pan_a**, **pan_b** (each oscillator's unison sum, before its own filter, so its stereo spread is kept) and **pan_sub** (the clean sub where it joins). Each is an equal-power balance, -1 left .. 1 right, unity in the centre (gains √2·cos / √2·sin of (v+1)π/4), glided per sample across a control block so a fast LFO doesn't click. No dial: LFO / matrix targets only.

### Any dial as an LFO target (owner's call, 26 Nov, as in Serum and Vital)

After the 62 fixed destinations every dest menu (`mod1-9.dest`, the custom LFOs' `dest`..`dest4`) lists
`p:<param id>` for every dial on the pages (449: OSC A / B's OCT-SEM (`semi`), FIN, Detune, Spread, Shape, Level,
Lo cut; the sub's OCT, Clip, Level; the noise's OCT, Tone, Density, Tail, Lo cut, Level; both envelopes' ADSR; the
filter's ENV, Cutoff, Res, Drive, ACCENT, VOWEL; LFO 1-4's knobs and the custom LFOs' knobs and Hz rate; every RUIN
knob (filter, bells, drive, EQ, phaser, the RUIN box's OTT / Lo cut / Mix / Output); every FX knob and the reverb's
OCT; Glide and Master). `K.MOD_TARGET_PARAMS` / `generated/ModTargets.h` hold their parameter indices (destination
`62 + k`). Each block `moveDials` (both engines) puts back last block's moved dials, smooths, then adds each
destination's amount (the newest voice's matrix with the custom LFOs, from the last block; the custom LFOs alone
while no voice sounds) to its dial in the dial's own 0..1 knob range (its curve), clamped, whole numbers rounded.

the dest menus (append only, so the numbers hold), and give its element `data-param` so the crosshair finds it.
