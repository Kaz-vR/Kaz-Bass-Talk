# Engine (Components 1: the synth engine)

*(Moved out of CLAUDE.md word for word to keep every session's context small; CLAUDE.md is the index. Read this file when a task touches it.)*

## Components

### 1. Synth engine
Up to 8 voices (`voice.polyphony`: 1 = monophonic with legato/glide, the bass default; 2-8 = polyphonic with voice stealing). Per voice:

- 2 main oscillators (sine, triangle, saw, pulse, wavetable with position, PWM-saw, physical-model string), each with detune, unison (1–7 voices), spread, level; no FM (owner's call: removed from both engines, its controls and its matrix destination; old patches' FM settings are ignored and the bank's FM keys, tines and bells play without it until the rebuild)
- Sub oscillator (sine, warm sine, triangle, square, pulse, saw; −4 to +4 octaves), always mono; noise. *(The click / attack transient layer was removed, owner's call. Fourteen types: seven beds and a hit of each, the menu in four columns, every hit in the same row as its bed (owner's call: no pitch and no rhythm anywhere; hum, machine and wind were tried and dropped, they load as rumble / air): GRAIN (crackle: vinyl pops only; dust; static: a steady grainy fizz with crackles, its flicker far too fast to hear as rhythm) · NOISE (tape hiss: steady, no flutter or dropouts; air; rumble; white noise: plain, straight up, the full range at Tone's top, Tone darkening it, Density its width) · HIT (crackle hit, dust hit, static hit) · HIT (tape hiss hit, air hit, rumble hit, white noise hit). A **hit** plays its bed's own generator inside one burst per new note, fading over Tail, nothing between, harder notes bursting bigger (`TEX_BED` in `src/engine/core/voicefx.ts`): a grain hit starts on its first grain (grains four times as dense), a noise hit swells in over ~10 ms. **Air hit is the breath** (shown as "air hit (breath)"): the puff on brass, bowed strings, flutes and keys; old presets' breath went where it leaned (`migrateBreath`: an attack chiff became air hit, Tail ~3× its decay; a sustained breath became the air bed). A **Density** dial (`texture.density`, 0.5 = each type's classic sound; the old Character is converted) on every type: on the grains how many (pops, ticks, crackles), on the noise how thick (how much of the range the band fills, its level held even). **Every type plays at the same loudness** wherever its Tone is: a trim table (`TEX_TRIM_DB`, K-weighted as LUFS, hits over their burst) made by `tools/level-texture.ts` and checked by `tests/texture.test.ts`; rerun the tool after changing a type's sound. An **OCT** box in the Noise title row (`texture.octave`, -6..+2: owner's pick after testing, -6 for experimental moments, above +2 changes little) plays the bed slower or faster like tape (the bed runs at 2^oct of the sample rate and is read back interpolated): down is darker and bigger (crackle becomes dark thumps, hiss sinks to rumble), up is smaller and quicker; the print stretches with it. A **KEY** lamp in the print's corner (`texture.key`) makes the noise **playable**: its speed follows the note like a sample across the keyboard (middle C at the OCT setting, an octave up twice as fast), with glide and pitch bend sliding it (glide from a high note to a low one for a tape stop; the HIT types become a noise kit across the keys), always within -6..+2; the bed is shared, so with chords it follows the newest note. The matrix's **Noise pitch** destination (`noise_pitch`, in the PITCH column of the destination menu, ±6 octaves at full amount, the newest voice's, as the FX targets) moves the speed on top: a drop LFO for a tape stop on every note, an LFO for a roar-to-fizz swing, velocity for a deeper dive. No combinations of types (owner's call).)*
- **GRIND** (`src/engine/core/grind.ts`, `grind.on` + `grind1..4.*`, off by default): switching it on starts from Destroyed Reese's chain (its values are the schema's GRIND defaults, owner's call; the other presets carry no GRIND values of their own until the rebuild); the neuro-bass chain, four stages **on the summed sound** (owner's calls: it sat before the main filter, where a dark preset's low-pass threw away most of what it made, and then inside each voice, where a chord could never grind against itself; now every voice (oscillators → mixer drive → pre-filter drive → main filter → amp) is summed, the noise bed added unless it is on Direct out or NOISE THRU, and the sum runs once through **RUIN → DRIVE → bitcrusher → tape** (`DirtBus`, `src/engine/core/bus.ts` / `plugin/engine/Bus.h`), RUIN and DRIVE 2x oversampled, always, so the latency never changes; the clean sub waits as long and joins after the effects; RUIN's KEY and ONE SHOT follow the newest note, the matrix's DRIVE / crush / tape targets the newest voice). **Mix** (`grind.mix`, dry / wet for the whole chain: the untouched input against the chain, OTT included, so Mix 0 is exactly the chain switched off), **Lo cut** (`grind.lowcut`: below it the sound passes clean, a 24 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley split), **KEY** (`grind.key`: every stage follows the note, Freq is where it sits at C3), **ONE SHOT** (`grind.oneshot`: stages moving with the note become an envelope: from the top of the range they fall fast and settle at the bottom until the next note, Rate shown as the sweep time in ms; the note movement restarts on every new note, legato included) and **OTT** (`grind.ott`, Depth: Ableton's OTT preset / Xfer's OTT, as extreme: three bands split at 88 Hz and 2.5 kHz, 1:66 down above and 4.17:1 up below the preset's thresholds, its band gains and times, the detector calibrated to the synth's bus level (+18 dB) with +15 dB makeup, so full Depth is louder and squashed with the tails pulled up; it sits on the whole synth after the amp envelopes, the voices and the noise bed before the effects, so it works on attacks and tails; the clean sub and everything under GRIND's Lo cut stay out; owner's call: the one exception to the no-compressor rule; neither OTT lifts anything below -80 dBFS (full lift from -70), so tails and residue never come back as hiss or ringing in silence). Nothing in the chain is level-matched (owner's call: push it hot): more Drive is louder and denser and hits the next stage harder, and RUIN's **Output** (`grind.output`, -24 … +12 dB, on the strip after Mix) sets the chain's level at the end. Each stage is a moving filter (notch, peak, band, comb, phaser: owner's pick, formant was dropped; levelled like EQ Eight (owner's call): wherever a filter isn't working it sits at exactly 0 dB and only the peak rises above it: notch flat with its dip, peak a bell up to +17 dB by Depth on a flat 0 dB, band's centre at 0 dB, comb's teeth at 0 dB with the dips between, phaser 0 dB away from its notches and its feedback peaks held to 0 dB) into its own drive, each switched off on its own (`grind<n>.filter_on` / `drive_on`, each box's thin on/off bar: FILTER n and DRIVE n, the stage on while either is, owner's call: a stage can be only its drive or only its filter) (owner's call: **tape** (highs pushed into a soft knee first by pre-emphasis and taken back after, a little top lost), **tube** (the lopsided tanh, even harmonics, a soft top), **amp** (a tightened input into a hot asymmetric preamp, a 4x12 cab through a close mic: low cut, a bump at 110 Hz, boxy mids scooped, bite at 2.4 kHz, a steep top at 5.2 kHz, then a few darker early reflections of a small room) **soft clip** (cubic), and the hard ones for neuro's bite (owner's call, under review): **hard clip**, **wavefold** (a sine fold), **rectify** (full-wave, the octave up), **downsample** (sample-and-hold, Drive taking the rate down to 1/128, no ADAA: aliasing on purpose) and **diode** (soft on top, clipping early below), and **starved** (owner's call, the Culture Vulture's starving as a type rather than a Bias dial: a tube that conducts only past a threshold, a dead zone around the centre, so quiet parts sputter, crackle and gate and only the big swings get through, lopsided; more Drive lifts more past it), every type but downsample followed by a DC blocker so no box pushes the next off centre; the curves' antiderivatives (ADAA) are taken from 0, so the types joined at 0 (rectify, diode, starved) stay continuous (before that fix rectify and diode jumped at every zero crossing: spikes, +40 dB, all distortion); the FX page's DRIVE has the same nine; their menu (owner's pick) is four columns by what they do, gentle to destroyed: WARM (tape, tube, starved, amp) · CLIP (soft clip, hard clip, diode) · BREAK (wavefold, rectify) · DIGITAL (downsample), each entry with a tiny picture of one sine cycle through it (`driveTypeSelector`, `driveIconPath`; the dropdown draws an option's `data-icon`); the defaults stage 1-4 tape, tube, amp, soft clip, Destroyed Reese's too; old patches' soft / hard load as soft clip, fold / fuzz as amp; ADAA on every curve). Each drive is laid out as Ableton's Overdrive (owner's call): a **band** in front of it (`grind<n>.drive_freq` 50 Hz-18 kHz, `drive_width` 0.5-9 octaves, 9 = the whole sound; owner's call, like a multiband plugin rather than Overdrive: a 24 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley split into below / the band / above, only the band driven and blended by Dry/Wet, below and above passing untouched and added back, so driving the top never loses the bottom (it used to: the band's outside was thrown away at full Dry/Wet); the FX page's DRIVE the same, `Stage.split`), shown as a curve with a ball you drag (sideways = where, up / down = how wide, double-click = full), then the drive, then **Tone** (`drive_tone`, a low-pass 200 Hz-20 kHz after it, fully up = open, its dotted top on the print); no level matching (`drive_dyn` / `dist.dyn` stay only so old patches load); the stage's Mix is its **Dry/Wet**. The drive box's dials: Drive, Tone, Dry/Wet (owner's call: the Bias dial and the OTT after each drive were removed as complexity; Bias lives on as the starved type, and the page's level meters show when a stage hits the next too hard). Drive (0 … +30 dB, even across the knob) on whatever level arrives (a hotter stage before it means more dirt here); with Mix; the filter moves around Freq by ±Range/2 octaves, moved at Rate, the clock (`grind<n>.clock`) being the movement (owner's call: the old NOTE / WANDER move switch was removed, since wander on Hz or Sync sounded like Rand anyway): **Hz** (an even swing up and down at exactly Rate, restarting from the top at every note), **Sync** (the same swing on the bar, 1 bar … 1/32; the old beat movement and `sync` load as Sync) or **Rand** (wander: smooth random, never repeating, each step at its own speed, up to ~3× either way around Rate; old patches with wander on Hz or Sync load as Rand; rolls pick Rand seven times out of ten, else Hz, never Sync: life, not the grid). ONE SHOT applies to the Hz and Sync swing only; its direction (`grind.oneshot_dir`, the arrow beside it, owner's call): down = from the top of the range to the bottom (right to left, the default), up = from the bottom to the top (left to right). Each filter has **Width** (`grind<n>.width`, owner's call: the old Res turned round, up = wider: a wider notch or band, a broader peak, softer comb teeth and phaser notches) and **Depth** (`grind<n>.depth`: how deep it goes, the filter blended in as 1 + Depth (H − 1): 0 = a straight line, no effect; the peak up to +17 dB at full Depth); only the frequency moves (owner's call: no vertical movement; V Range and the wandering resonance were removed, old patches load Res as Width and keep Depth full). Each stage's live frequency goes to the page on worklet control channels 12-15 and 24-27 (stages 5-8; their resonance 16-19 and 28-31; the worklet's control output has 32 channels, the plugin's `live` array 32 too) (`EngineHost.liveGrind`). A DC blocker ends the chain. About 3.5 % CPU per voice with all four stages (meant for mono basses; not optimized yet). In both engines (`plugin/engine/Grind.h`).
- **Mixer drive** (`drive.mixer`, the MIX glow number under ENV in the Filter box; part of the drive stage): the oscillators' sum overdriving first, the Minimoog way (tanh with ADAA, gain 1 + 8·amount, make-up 1/√gain: louder mixes and chords get denser), before GRIND and the pre-filter drive; 0 = off
- **Oct down** (`voice.halfspeed`, a lamp on the play strip after Legato; owner's call): the whole synth at half speed, live, as a frozen render pitched down an octave in Ableton's Repitch mode (everything an octave lower and darker, envelopes, glides, LFOs and effects twice as slow, notes still starting when played): the engine renders one sample for every two played, read back through a windowed-sinc 2× interpolator, then the output's safety stages again at the real rate (subsonic / DC filter); it switches at the bottom of the engine's duck, held silent until the interpolator has emptied (no click); synced clocks free-run at half the host's tempo while it is on. Both engines.
- Pre-filter drive → filter (4-pole low-pass, band-pass, formant (vowels, for choir), 303 diode with accent, notch, comb, low-pass gate, 4-pole high-pass: sweep it up to thin a sound out, and **SEM** (`sem`, owner's call: an Oberheim SEM-style 12 dB low-pass, a bending state-variable filter, softer and more open than the ladder, keeping its bass when resonant)) → **DRIVE** (owner's call: the FX page's distortion became RUIN's drive, `DriveUnit` in `grind.ts` / `Grind.h`, the same code a RUIN stage runs: `dist.type` tape / tube / amp / soft clip, the Overdrive band (`dist.band_freq`, `dist.band_width`) on a pad you drag, drawn like a RUIN drive (shaded band, a ring, Tone as a dotted line), Drive, Tone (`dist.post_tone`, fully up = open), Dry/Wet (`dist.mix`), **Output** (`dist.output`, -24 … +12 dB: it is pushed hot, not level-matched); old patches' soft loads as tape, hard as soft clip, fold / fuzz as amp; `dist.clean_below` keeps the part below it clean), on the summed voices (see GRIND above) → bitcrusher → tape; in each voice: amp → **mid clip** (transparent soft clipper on osc 1 + 2, 0–12 dB drive, 0 = off)
- **Filter slots per source:** each oscillator has its own filter before the main filter (high/low 24 or 12 dB, band, keytrack; high 24 at 20 Hz = off, the default); the sub and the texture bed have one too (off by default). **Routing into the main filter:** A and B (on by default; off = that oscillator skips the drive and main filter), N (the texture bed through a copy of the main filter following the newest note) and S (the sub through the main filter and the rest of the voice). Main filter **Mix** (dry/filtered; only under "All controls"). *(Fat was removed, owner's call.)*
- **Sub:** sine, warm sine (a touch of its octave: round but heard on small speakers), triangle, square, pulse (25%, the Juno / SH-101 sub) or saw (the richest), the edges band-limited with polyBLEP; owner's call: no shape dial, the menu carries the colours; its octave -4..+4 against the note (owner's call: -1 / -2 was too limiting; below 20 Hz folds up, 0 and up make it a clean mono layer at or above the note); **Direct Out** on (default) = its own clean path, off = through the effects.
- **Sub clip:** the sub's own transparent soft clipper (0–12 dB, 0 = off) on its clean path. Both clippers leave everything under the knee at its level (denser, not louder: the level matching is built in)
- **Analog modelling (owner's call, after comparing with the V Collection / Diva):** the 4-pole low-pass is a saturating transistor ladder (its input / resonance stage and each of its four poles bend, `LADDER_V`; each pole solved with its gain linearised around the last sample, Mystran's method, so it stays zero-delay: loud signals and high resonance thicken and squash, the bass sags with resonance); the 12 dB filters (band, notch, high-pass, low-pass gate) bend at their input and resonance (`Svf.processSat`, `SVF_V`); the saws are bowed like a charging capacitor and pulses droop and drift in width, by Analog; in poly every voice has its own **voice card** (fixed offsets in tuning ±10 ct, cutoff ±0.3 oct, envelope times ±20 %, level ±1 dB, all times the Analog amount; mono keeps one tuning against its sub) and free voices rotate, so repeated notes land on different cards; in poly every note's envelope times also differ a touch (±12 % × Analog; mono keeps the same punch on every note). The **Analog** amount is the Drift dial in the FX page's Tape box (`voice.analog`). Both engines.
- Amp and mod envelopes, each segment with its own bend (`<env>.attack_bend`, `decay_bend`, `release_bend`, -1..1; 0 = straight, up = fast first (analog, the defaults), down = a swell; the old attack shape is converted); per-note random, analog drift, free unison phases
- Instrument additions (phase 5): delayed vibrato, breath/noise attack, richer formant vowels, inharmonic bell partials with separate decays, long riser/fall envelopes

Shared by all voices: 6 LFOs (tempo-sync, retrigger, fade-in; shapes sine, triangle, saws, square, S&H, **HUMANIZE** (`wander` inside, so patches keep loading; owner's call: as a player wobbles, slow and fast at once, never a pendulum: slow moves each with its own length, a third of a cycle to three, mostly small nudges and now and then a big one, plus a tremble six times as fast at a quarter of the depth, just as uneven; Rate is how often on average; both engines), **drift** and **slow drift** (the old matrix sources as shapes, moving exactly as they did: drift glides to a new random point every 0.4-1.6 s at 1 Hz, slow drift curves between random points every 2-6 s at 0.25 Hz; picking one sets that rate, Rate scales it; now their movement shows with the dot) and three **per-note** shapes that each voice runs on its own from the note's start: **bloom** (rises from 0 to full over one cycle and holds: the slow bloom, which replaced Env 2), **drop** (falls from full to 0 over one cycle: 808 drops, tape stops, plucks; it replaced Env 3) and **random** (a new random value each note, held: the old Random source); owner's call: the LFO page's left side is only LFOs), the **MOD MATRIX**, 9 rows, all the preset's own (owner's call; per-voice sources are evaluated per voice; FX targets follow the newest voice; it was 12, and older patches' working rows move up into the nine on load, `migrateMatrix`; its bar is the master switch). **RUIN'S LFO**, four built-in life rows (HUMAN PITCH / SWELL / TONE / GRIT on two wander LFOs), was tried and removed (owner's call: barely audible to a user, where RUIN, Wow and Drift are dials you turn up and hear, and Drift already moves each voice on its own; patches from then load without those rows and the LFOs only they used switched off); a preset's life comes from its own LFOs (HUMANIZE) and the tape, the **mod wheel moves** (8 connected recipes, `WHEEL_MOVES` in `src/engine/core/params.ts`; each preset names its own in `wheel.preset`, by category in `data/categories.json`), the texture bed (six beds and their hits, with a Density dial, see Noise above; follows the notes with its own tail, silent between notes), and the FX in a **fixed order**: chorus (classic, ensemble, **BBD** (`bbd`, owner's call: the Juno / Jupiter chorus, one bucket-brigade delay per side swept by a triangle in opposite directions, darkened at ~7 kHz, softly saturated, a faint hiss) or **hyper**: six steadily detuned copies spread left to right, Serum's Hyper as a chorus mode, a supersaw thickness on the whole sound) → flanger → phaser (each in its own box) → **reverb** (back in its box, owner's call; the FX page's OTT was taken out again, RUIN keeps its own; the `ott.*` parameters stay only so old patches load) → EQ. **The delay was taken out (owner's call: every producer uses their own)**: no longer heard, no box, its parameters kept only so old presets load. (The reverb: a vintage digital reverb in the manner of Valhalla VintageVerb's 1970s colour: dark, band-limited to ~7.5 kHz, a diffused input into an eight-line chorused feedback delay network; types room, plate, hall, cathedral (`reverb.type`), Decay as RT60 seconds (`reverb.decay`, 0.2-20 s, the level evened out across it), Pre-delay (`reverb.predelay`, 0-250 ms), an **OCT** box in its title row (`reverb.octave`, 0 / -1: -1 shifts the reverb's input an octave down before its Lo cut, so a note sinks into a dark cloud an octave below itself; `OctaveDown` in `src/engine/core/fx.ts`); dials Mix, Decay, Pre-delay, Lo cut; the old Size / Tone are converted by `validatePatch`) (each only above its own low cut, `<fx>.lowcut`) → EQ (four bands like Ableton's EQ Eight: a low shelf that can become a high-pass, two bells that can become notches, a high shelf that can become a low-pass; `eq.b1..4_type/_freq/_gain/_q`, flat by default, every gain times **Scale** (`eq.scale`, -200 … 200 %); the old low / mid / high knobs load as the same bands) + high cut (only under "All controls"; owner's call: every preset's EQ is flat, all bands at 0 dB and the high cut open at 20 kHz, nothing taking the top off; the rebuilt bank keeps it flat too) → **+ the clean sub** (its own path: it skips effects and EQ; only the Sub box shapes it) → subsonic/DC filter → out. No limiter and no forced mono low end (owner's call, removed completely: the preset and the producer decide).

Quality: band-limited oscillators, oversampled anti-aliased nonlinear stages. Expensive-sounding, never "chiptune" unless deliberate. Every parameter is defined in the patch schema (~180 today).
