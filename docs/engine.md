## Components

### 1. Synth engine
Up to 8 voices (`voice.polyphony`: 1 = monophonic with legato/glide, the bass default; 2-8 = polyphonic with voice stealing). Per voice:

- 2 main oscillators (sine, triangle, saw, pulse, wavetable with position (shown as MORPH), PWM-saw, physical-model string; and (owner's call, 27 Nov) **square** (a true square at Shape 0, every odd harmonic up to 1023 at exactly 1/k, built from its spectrum, `squareCycle`; Shape rounds it towards a sine), **double saw, sync saw, vowel, choir, talk, bell, ring, digital, glass, bowed, reed, breath, tape saw, cassette pulse, lofi sample, broken sine, electric guitar**, and (owner's call, 27 Nov) the real **wavetables**, made to be scanned with Shape (9 keyframes, 2 frames a step): **acid**, **scream**, **growl** (a neuro yoi), **fold**, **harmonic** (partials one by one), **sync sweep** (`sync saw`), **crush**, **spectral** (a moving comb), **formant scan**, **gritty**; (owner's call, 27 Nov: exactly Serum's) **acid, ring, scream and gritty are sampled**: Serum's own tables, frame by frame from the owner's renders (`tools/waves/*.wav`, 2048 samples a frame: acid 40 frames, ring 33, scream 123, gritty 4); and (owner's call, 27 Nov, after learning from them: made the way Serum's tables are, not as formulas) **every other bank wave but square and harmonic is processed**: a real source through real processing, one cycle at 16x resolution then cut to 1023 harmonics, full band, 32-128 frames each a little different from its neighbours, the frames keeping their natural levels (`tools/build-waves.py`: glottal pulses through a parallel formant bank for vowel / choir / talk / formant scan and the MORPH table's vocal end, a phase-modulated saw through moving narrow formants driven hard for growl, FM pairs for bell, bit operations for digital, windowed sync for glass, Helmholtz bow motion and a wooden body for bowed, a soft-clipped reed for reed, a flute jet for breath, a plucked string, tube preamp, tone stack and cab for e-guitar, a tape chain (emphasis, asymmetric saturation, head bump, head loss, phase smear) for tape saw and cassette pulse, a sampler for lofi sample and crush, wavefolders for broken sine and fold, continuous hard sync for sync sweep, a moving comb for spectral); both taken by `tools/extract-waves.py` (DC removed per frame, the whole table scaled by one number, int16 in `wave-tables.ts` and `generated/WaveTables.h`), one frame per Shape step, not peak-normalised per frame (`BANK_SAMPLED`): each a small morph table of the **wave bank** (`BANK` / `bankKey` in `wavetable.ts`, `Wavetable.h`: 3 or 5 keyframes as one cycle in time, FFT band-limited and mip-mapped like the morph table, built at start-up after it, ~1.2 s, ~56 MB of RAM, nothing on disk), Shape (`wt_pos`) moving through its frames; every one is only its table, nothing layered or added (owner's call: no hidden effects, so the supersaw and the breath's noise were taken out again: saw with UNI 7 is the supersaw, the Noise box's air bed the breath), each with detune, unison (1–7 voices), spread, level; no FM (owner's call: removed from both engines, its controls and its matrix destination; old patches' FM settings are ignored and the bank's FM keys, tines and bells play without it until the rebuild)
- Sub oscillator (sine, warm sine, triangle, square, pulse, saw; −4 to +4 octaves), always mono; noise. *(The click / attack transient layer was removed, owner's call. Fourteen types: seven beds and a hit of each, the menu in four columns, every hit in the same row as its bed (owner's call: no pitch and no rhythm anywhere; hum, machine and wind were tried and dropped, they load as rumble / air): GRAIN (crackle: vinyl pops only; dust; static: a steady grainy fizz with crackles, its flicker far too fast to hear as rhythm) · NOISE (tape hiss: steady, no flutter or dropouts; air; rumble; white noise: plain, straight up, the full range at Tone's top, Tone darkening it, Density its width) · HIT (crackle hit, dust hit, static hit) · HIT (tape hiss hit, air hit, rumble hit, white noise hit). A **hit** plays its bed's own generator inside one burst per new note, fading over Tail, nothing between, harder notes bursting bigger (`TEX_BED` in `src/engine/core/voicefx.ts`): a grain hit starts on its first grain (grains four times as dense), a noise hit swells in over ~10 ms. **Air hit is the breath** (shown as "air hit (breath)"): the puff on brass, bowed strings, flutes and keys; old presets' breath went where it leaned (`migrateBreath`: an attack chiff became air hit, Tail ~3× its decay; a sustained breath became the air bed). A **Density** dial (`texture.density`, 0.5 = each type's classic sound; the old Character is converted) on every type: on the grains how many (pops, ticks, crackles), on the noise how thick (how much of the range the band fills, its level held even). **Every type plays at the same loudness** wherever its Tone is: a trim table (`TEX_TRIM_DB`, K-weighted as LUFS, hits over their burst) made by `tools/level-texture.ts` and checked by `tests/texture.test.ts`; rerun the tool after changing a type's sound. An **OCT** box in the Noise title row (`texture.octave`, -6..+2: owner's pick after testing, -6 for experimental moments, above +2 changes little) plays the bed slower or faster like tape (the bed runs at 2^oct of the sample rate and is read back interpolated): down is darker and bigger (crackle becomes dark thumps, hiss sinks to rumble), up is smaller and quicker; the print stretches with it. A **KEY** lamp in the print's corner (`texture.key`) makes the noise **playable**: its speed follows the note like a sample across the keyboard (middle C at the OCT setting, an octave up twice as fast), with glide and pitch bend sliding it (glide from a high note to a low one for a tape stop; the HIT types become a noise kit across the keys), always within -6..+2; the bed is shared, so with chords it follows the newest note. The matrix's **Noise pitch** destination (`noise_pitch`, in the PITCH column of the destination menu, ±6 octaves at full amount, the newest voice's, as the FX targets) moves the speed on top: a drop LFO for a tape stop on every note, an LFO for a roar-to-fizz swing, velocity for a deeper dive. No combinations of types (owner's call).)*
- **GRIND** (`src/engine/core/grind.ts`, `grind.on` + `grind1..4.*`, off by default): switching it on starts from Destroyed Reese's chain (its values are the schema's GRIND defaults, owner's call; the other presets carry no GRIND values of their own until the rebuild); the neuro-bass chain, four stages **on the summed sound** (owner's calls: it sat before the main filter, where a dark preset's low-pass threw away most of what it made, and then inside each voice, where a chord could never grind against itself; now every voice (oscillators → pre-filter drive → main filter → amp) is summed, the noise bed added unless it is on Direct out or NOISE THRU, and the sum runs once through **RUIN → DRIVE → bitcrusher → tape** (`DirtBus`, `src/engine/core/bus.ts` / `plugin/engine/Bus.h`), RUIN and DRIVE 2x oversampled, always, so the latency never changes; the clean sub waits as long and joins after the effects; RUIN's KEY and ONE SHOT follow the newest note, the matrix's DRIVE / crush / tape targets the newest voice). **Mix** (`grind.mix`, dry / wet for the whole chain: the untouched input against the chain, OTT included, so Mix 0 is exactly the chain switched off), **Lo cut** (`grind.lowcut`: below it the sound passes clean, a 24 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley split), **KEY** (`grind.key`: every stage follows the note, Freq is where it sits at C3), **ONE SHOT** (`grind.oneshot`: stages moving with the note become an envelope: from the top of the range they fall fast and settle at the bottom until the next note, Rate shown as the sweep time in ms; the note movement restarts on every new note, legato included) and **OTT** (`grind.ott`, Depth: Ableton's OTT preset / Xfer's OTT, as extreme: three bands split at 88 Hz and 2.5 kHz, 1:66 down above and 4.17:1 up below the preset's thresholds, its band gains and times, the detector calibrated to the synth's bus level (+18 dB) with +15 dB makeup, so full Depth is louder and squashed with the tails pulled up; it sits on the whole synth after the amp envelopes, the voices and the noise bed before the effects, so it works on attacks and tails; the clean sub and everything under GRIND's Lo cut stay out; owner's call: the one exception to the no-compressor rule; neither OTT lifts anything below -80 dBFS (full lift from -70), so tails and residue never come back as hiss or ringing in silence). Nothing in the chain is level-matched (owner's call: push it hot): more Drive is louder and denser and hits the next stage harder, and RUIN's **Output** (`grind.output`, -24 … +12 dB, on the strip after Mix) sets the chain's level at the end. **No ONE SHOT** (owner's call, 24 Nov): both engines ignore `grind.oneshot` / `oneshot_dir` (kept in the schema only so old patches load), every stage always moves on its clock; the old filter types stay in the engine only for old patches, the bank and the page use BELLS alone. **PHASER parts** (owner's call, `phaser 1-8` in the boxes, `grind<n>.phaser_on/_level`, `ph_*`): up to 12 notches (`ph_notches`), each a second-order all-pass (SVF, Q 1.1) at CENTER ± SPREAD octaves apart, swept together AMOUNT octaves by the part's own LFO (`ph_clock`: HZ free running, SYNC on the bar, RANDOM wandering; `ph_rate`), the right channel STEREO degrees ahead (on RANDOM a blend towards its own path), FEEDBACK (0-0.9) around the all-passes, the dry + all-passes levelled as the old phaser filter; its live L / R LFO goes to the page packed (`packPhaser`) on the worklet's 4th output (8 channels; the plugin's live values 32-39). Each stage is a moving filter (notch, peak, band, comb, phaser, **bells**: owner's pick, formant was dropped; **bells** (owner's call) is the filter as an EQ: four bands in a row, each with its own **type** as EQ Eight's eight (`grind<n>.bell1..4_type`, owner's call: low cut 48 / 12 dB, low shelf, bell (default), notch, high shelf, high cut 12 / 48 dB; one SVF mixed c0·x + c1·bp + c2·lp + c3·hp, k = 1/Q, a bell x + (gain − 1)·k·bp, the shelves after Simper (the corner moved by √A so the gain's middle sits at Freq), the 48 dB cuts four Butterworth sections, the last one's Q times Q / 0.71; Gain works on the bell and the shelves; and the old filters as band types (owner's call): band (only the band round Freq, `k·bp`), comb (a delay of one period of Freq, Gain = feedback ±0.92, minus = hollow, its peaks at 0 dB) and phaser (four first-order all-passes at Freq with feedback 0.85 × log2(1+Q)/log2(19), mixed with the dry); `grind<n>.bell_count` 1-4 = how many bands are used), Freq, Gain (±18 dB), Q and range (`_freq/_gain/_q/_range`), each moving in its own zone (its Freq × KEY ± half its range, slid inside 20 Hz-20 kHz) on the box's Rate and clock, each on its own path (HZ / SYNC a quarter cycle apart, RANDOM each wandering on its own; ONE SHOT sweeps them together), each band in its own **direction** (owner's call, 26 Nov: `grind<n>.bell<k>_dir` left right = its frequency moves in its range, up down = its gain moves ± half its up down range `bell<k>_vrange` (0-36 dB) around its Gain, cross = both on the same path; moving up down only, band 1's live value carries its position as an octave around its Freq for the page), or with **LINK** (`grind<n>.bell_link`, owner's call) all on band 1's path, together (a vowel's formants); no Width or Depth; the page reads bells 2-4's positions packed into the stage's live resonance value (`packBells`); levelled like EQ Eight (owner's call): wherever a filter isn't working it sits at exactly 0 dB and only the peak rises above it: notch flat with its dip, peak a bell up to +17 dB by Depth on a flat 0 dB, band's centre at 0 dB, comb's teeth at 0 dB with the dips between, phaser 0 dB away from its notches and its feedback peaks held to 0 dB) into its own drive, each switched off on its own (`grind<n>.filter_on` / `drive_on`, each box's thin on/off bar: FILTER n and DRIVE n, the stage on while either is, owner's call: a stage can be only its drive or only its filter) (owner's call: **tape** (highs pushed into a soft knee first by pre-emphasis and taken back after, a little top lost), **tube** (the lopsided tanh, even harmonics, a soft top), **amp** (a tightened input into a hot asymmetric preamp, a 4x12 cab through a close mic: low cut, a bump at 110 Hz, boxy mids scooped, bite at 2.4 kHz, a steep top at 5.2 kHz, then a few darker early reflections of a small room) **soft clip** (cubic), and the hard ones for neuro's bite (owner's call, under review): **hard clip**, **wavefold** (a sine fold), **rectify** (full-wave, the octave up), **downsample** (sample-and-hold, Drive taking the rate down to 1/128, no ADAA: aliasing on purpose) and **diode** (soft on top, clipping early below), and **starved** (owner's call, the Culture Vulture's starving as a type rather than a Bias dial: a tube that conducts only past a threshold, a dead zone around the centre, so quiet parts sputter, crackle and gate and only the big swings get through, lopsided; more Drive lifts more past it), every type but downsample followed by a DC blocker so no box pushes the next off centre; the curves' antiderivatives (ADAA) are taken from 0, so the types joined at 0 (rectify, diode, starved) stay continuous (before that fix rectify and diode jumped at every zero crossing: spikes, +40 dB, all distortion); the FX page's DRIVE has the same nine; their menu (owner's pick) is four columns by what they do, gentle to destroyed: WARM (tape, tube, starved, amp) · CLIP (soft clip, hard clip, diode) · BREAK (wavefold, rectify) · DIGITAL (downsample), each entry with a tiny picture of one sine cycle through it (`driveTypeSelector`, `driveIconPath`; the dropdown draws an option's `data-icon`); the defaults stage 1-4 tape, tube, amp, soft clip, Destroyed Reese's too; old patches' soft / hard load as soft clip, fold / fuzz as amp; ADAA on every curve). Each drive is laid out as Ableton's Overdrive (owner's call): a **band** in front of it (`grind<n>.drive_freq` 50 Hz-18 kHz, `drive_width` 0.5-9 octaves, 9 = the whole sound; owner's call, like a multiband plugin rather than Overdrive: a 24 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley split into below / the band / above, only the band driven and blended by Dry/Wet, below and above passing untouched and added back, so driving the top never loses the bottom (it used to: the band's outside was thrown away at full Dry/Wet); the FX page's DRIVE the same, `Stage.split`), shown as a curve with a ball you drag (sideways = where, up / down = how wide, double-click = full), then the drive, then **Tone** (`drive_tone`, a low-pass 200 Hz-20 kHz after it, fully up = open, its dotted top on the print); no level matching (`drive_dyn` / `dist.dyn` stay only so old patches load); the stage's Mix is its **Dry/Wet**. The drive box's dials: Drive, Tone, Dry/Wet (owner's call: the Bias dial and the OTT after each drive were removed as complexity; Bias lives on as the starved type, and the page's level meters show when a stage hits the next too hard). Drive (0 … +30 dB, even across the knob) on whatever level arrives (a hotter stage before it means more dirt here); with Mix; the filter moves around Freq by ±Range/2 octaves, moved at Rate, the clock (`grind<n>.clock`) being the movement (owner's call: the old NOTE / WANDER move switch was removed, since wander on Hz or Sync sounded like Rand anyway): **Hz** (an even swing up and down at exactly Rate, restarting from the top at every note), **Sync** (the same swing on the bar, 1 bar … 1/32; the old beat movement and `sync` load as Sync) or **Rand** (wander: smooth random, never repeating, each step at its own speed, up to ~3× either way around Rate; old patches with wander on Hz or Sync load as Rand; rolls pick Rand seven times out of ten, else Hz, never Sync: life, not the grid). ONE SHOT (one sweep per note that holds at its end) works on all three clocks, on Rand each note's sweep at its own speed around Rate; its direction (`grind.oneshot_dir`, the arrow beside it, owner's call): down = from the top of the range to the bottom (right to left, the default), up = from the bottom to the top (left to right). Each filter has **Width** (`grind<n>.width`, owner's call: the old Res turned round, up = wider: a wider notch or band, a broader peak, softer comb teeth and phaser notches) and **Depth** (`grind<n>.depth`: how deep it goes, the filter blended in as 1 + Depth (H − 1): 0 = a straight line, no effect; the peak up to +17 dB at full Depth); only the frequency moves (owner's call: no vertical movement; V Range and the wandering resonance were removed, old patches load Res as Width and keep Depth full). Each stage's live frequency goes to the page on worklet control channels 12-15 and 24-27 (stages 5-8; their resonance 16-19 and 28-31; the worklet's control output has 32 channels, the plugin's `live` array 32 too) (`EngineHost.liveGrind`). A DC blocker ends the chain. About 3.5 % CPU per voice with all four stages (meant for mono basses; not optimized yet). In both engines (`plugin/engine/Grind.h`).
- **Oct down** (`voice.halfspeed`, a lamp on the play strip after Legato; owner's call): the whole synth at half speed, live, as a frozen render pitched down an octave in Ableton's Repitch mode (everything an octave lower and darker, envelopes, glides, LFOs and effects twice as slow, notes still starting when played): the engine renders one sample for every two played, read back through a windowed-sinc 2× interpolator, then the output's safety stages again at the real rate (subsonic / DC filter); it switches at the bottom of the engine's duck, held silent until the interpolator has emptied (no click); synced clocks free-run at half the host's tempo while it is on. Both engines.
- Pre-filter drive → filter (4-pole low-pass, band-pass, formant (vowels, for choir), 303 diode with accent, notch, comb, low-pass gate, 4-pole high-pass: sweep it up to thin a sound out; (owner's call, 27 Nov) **low-pass 12** (a clean 12 dB SVF), **MS-20** (a 12 dB low-pass driven hard in and out, resonance near screaming), **phaser** (four all-passes at Cutoff with the dry, Res feeding back), **ring** (times a sine at Cutoff Hz, Res how much), **decimate** (held at Cutoff as a sample rate, Res taking the bits from 16 to 3), **talk** (the formant filter with Cutoff sweeping the vowel, the throat fixed); and **SEM** (`sem`, owner's call: an Oberheim SEM-style 12 dB low-pass, a bending state-variable filter, softer and more open than the ladder, keeping its bass when resonant)) → **DRIVE** (owner's call: the FX page's distortion became RUIN's drive, `DriveUnit` in `grind.ts` / `Grind.h`, the same code a RUIN stage runs: `dist.type` tape / tube / amp / soft clip, the Overdrive band (`dist.band_freq`, `dist.band_width`) on a pad you drag, drawn like a RUIN drive (shaded band, a ring, Tone as a dotted line), Drive, Tone (`dist.post_tone`, fully up = open), Dry/Wet (`dist.mix`), **Output** (`dist.output`, -24 … +12 dB: it is pushed hot, not level-matched); old patches' soft loads as tape, hard as soft clip, fold / fuzz as amp; `dist.clean_below` keeps the part below it clean), on the summed voices (see GRIND above) → bitcrusher → tape; in each voice: amp → **mid clip** (transparent soft clipper on osc 1 + 2, 0–12 dB drive, 0 = off)
- **Filter slots per source:** each oscillator has its own filter before the main filter (high/low 24 or 12 dB, band, keytrack; high 24 at 20 Hz = off, the default); the sub and the texture bed have one too (off by default). **Routing into the main filter:** A and B (on by default; off = that oscillator skips the drive and main filter), N (the texture bed through a copy of the main filter following the newest note) and S (the sub through the main filter and the rest of the voice). Main filter **Mix** (dry/filtered; only under "All controls"). *(Fat was removed, owner's call.)*
- **Sub:** sine, warm sine (a touch of its octave: round but heard on small speakers), triangle, square, pulse (25%, the Juno / SH-101 sub) or saw (the richest), the edges band-limited with polyBLEP; owner's call: no shape dial, the menu carries the colours; its octave -4..+4 against the note (owner's call: -1 / -2 was too limiting; below 20 Hz folds up, 0 and up make it a clean mono layer at or above the note); **Direct Out** on (default) = its own clean path, off = through the effects.
- **Sub clip:** the sub's own transparent soft clipper (0–12 dB, 0 = off) on its clean path. Both clippers leave everything under the knee at its level (denser, not louder: the level matching is built in)
- **Analog modelling (owner's call, after comparing with the V Collection / Diva):** the 4-pole low-pass is a saturating transistor ladder (its input / resonance stage and each of its four poles bend, `LADDER_V`; each pole solved with its gain linearised around the last sample, Mystran's method, so it stays zero-delay: loud signals and high resonance thicken and squash, the bass sags with resonance); the 12 dB filters (band, notch, high-pass, low-pass gate) bend at their input and resonance (`Svf.processSat`, `SVF_V`); the saws are bowed like a charging capacitor and pulses droop and drift in width, by Analog; in poly every voice has its own **voice card** (fixed offsets in tuning ±10 ct, cutoff ±0.3 oct, envelope times ±20 %, level ±1 dB, all times the Analog amount; mono keeps one tuning against its sub) and free voices rotate, so repeated notes land on different cards; in poly every note's envelope times also differ a touch (±12 % × Analog; mono keeps the same punch on every note). The **Analog** amount is the Drift dial in the FX page's Tape box (`voice.analog`). Both engines.
- Amp and mod envelopes, each segment with its own bend (`<env>.attack_bend`, `decay_bend`, `release_bend`, -1..1; 0 = straight, up = fast first (analog, the defaults), down = a swell; the old attack shape is converted); per-note random, analog drift, free unison phases
- Instrument additions (phase 5): delayed vibrato, breath/noise attack, richer formant vowels, inharmonic bell partials with separate decays, long riser/fall envelopes

each preset names its own in `wheel.preset`, by category in `data/categories.json`), the texture bed (six beds and their hits, with a Density dial, see Noise above; follows the notes with its own tail, silent between notes), and the FX in a **fixed order** (owner's call, 26 Nov: chorus → **HYPER / DIMENSION** → **delay** → reverb → EQ; Expensive-sounding, never "chiptune" unless deliberate. Every parameter is defined in the patch schema (~180 today).


### VIBRATO (LFO shapes 13-17, owner's call: a realistic vibrato, played with the mod wheel)

Sine (13, the classic), triangle (14, from 0 like the sine), saw up (15), saw down (16) or square (17) (`vibWave`),
running per note (restarting with each note), never on without the wheel: its width is the mod wheel squared
(the first half gentle) times its own Depth (`lfo<n>.vib_depth`: 0 to ±50 cents on a pitch target, 0-100 % like an
LFO's Depth on any other (`isPitchDest`); the matrix slot's amount is ignored for a VIBRATO source), at its own Rate (`lfo<n>.vib_rate`, 3-12 Hz, or synced), which the
wheel speeds up slightly (x 0.9 at the bottom to x 1.1 at the top). No LFO fades in any more (`lfo<n>.fade` is unused,
kept so old presets load). Both engines (`voice.ts`, `Voice.h`).

The mod wheel plays only the VIBRATO shapes (owner's call): its eight connected moves were removed from `extraModulation` in both engines.

### The LFO page's filters (owner's call, 24 Nov)

Six matrix destinations of their own (`D_LF_ALL`..`D_LF_NOISE`, 47-52: `lfo_filter`, `lfo_filter_ab`, `lfo_filter_a`,
`lfo_filter_b`, `lfo_filter_sub`, `lfo_filter_noise`), apart from the OSC page's filter and its A / B / S / N lamps:
12 dB low-passes (`Svf12`, Simper's SVF at Q 0.707) on the whole voice after everything (the sub included, at its
output), on A, on B (A + B = both), on the sub and on the noise. Each is open (20 kHz) at the top of its routes' swing
and 8 octaves lower at the bottom: closed by `LF_OCTAVES / 2 x (swing - value)` octaves, the swing being the sum of its
routes' |amount| (a VIBRATO's times the wheel squared), so no route, Depth 0 or a vibrato at wheel 0 leaves the sound
untouched. RESONANCE stays the OSC page filter's. The vibrato's value is smoothed over ~3 ms so its square and saws
never click on a volume or filter target. LFO routes to the OSC page's cutoff were moved to FILTER
(`tools/convert-lfo-filters.ts`). Both engines.

The custom LFO's HZ mode (`wobble.free`, `wobble.hz`): its cycle is `tempo / 60 / hz` beats, so everything else (one shot, shift, random steps) runs as in SYNC. Both engines.

### RUIN drive targets and the custom LFO's targets (owner's call, 25 Nov)

`ruin_drives` (53) moves every RUIN drive, `ruin_drive1`..`8` (54-61) the drive in that RUIN box; each relative to its
own Drive (`drive x (1 + value)`, clamped 0-1), its gain glided (`gainT`, LV_SMOOTH) so it never steps; the bus hands
the newest voice's matrix to `Grind.control`. FX DRIVE is `dist_drive` (added to its Drive). The custom LFO has three
targets (`wobble.dest`, `dest2`, `dest3`) with signed depths (`wobble.amt1-3`, -1..1): FILTER (`lfo_filter`) = its own
filter, VOLUME (`amp`) = its own gate (minus = the shape upside down), the rest `v x amt` into every voice's matrix.
`wobble.depth`, `wobble.cutoff`, `wobble.volume` are unused now (old patches migrate in `migrateWobTargets`).

**Four custom LFOs** (owner's call, 25 Nov): `wobble.*` and `wobble2.*` .. `wobble4.*`, each its own shape, rate,
one shot, Smooth, shift and **four targets** (`dest`..`dest4`, `amt1-4`, `offset1-4`), run by `WobUnit`
(`src/engine/core/wobunit.ts`, `plugin/engine/WobUnit.h`), each with its own random source. Each target follows
**its own line**: `y = master x (depth x (v - 0.5) + offset / 2)`, so depth 100 % = the shape itself (a new
target's start), 0 = flat, -100 % = upside down, Offset (starting at 0) moves the line up or down by up to half the
range, and the **master Depth** (`wobble.master`, 0-1, default 1) scales all of them (0 = the LFO does nothing). A
matrix target moves by `y` around where it is set; the FILTER lines of all four multiply into their one own filter
(a 24 dB low-pass open at the top of the line, shut at 60 Hz at its bottom), the VOLUME lines into their one gate
(the level). **No Lo cut** (owner's call: it only made sense for Filter and Volume; `wobble.lowcut` removed from
both engines and the schema), so the gate and filter work on the whole sound. Their phases go to the page two to a
value (`packWob`, worklet control channels 2 and 11, the plugin's live values 2 and 3). Older patches are converted
to the same sound (`migrateWobOffsets`: matrix offset = depth x o, filter / gate offset = o x (1 - |depth|), o the
old `wobble.off` or +1). Both engines.

### Any dial as an LFO target (owner's call, 26 Nov, as in Serum and Vital)

After the 62 fixed destinations every dest menu (`mod1-9.dest`, the custom LFOs' `dest`..`dest4`) lists
`p:<param id>` for every dial on the pages (449: OSC A / B's OCT-SEM (`semi`), FIN, Detune, Spread, Shape, Level,
Lo cut; the sub's OCT, Clip, Level; the noise's OCT, Tone, Density, Tail, Lo cut, Level; both envelopes' ADSR; the
filter's ENV, Cutoff, Res, Drive, ACCENT, VOWEL; LFO 1-4's knobs and the custom LFOs' knobs and Hz rate; every RUIN
knob (filter, bells, drive, EQ, phaser, the RUIN box's OTT / Lo cut / Mix / Output); every FX knob and the reverb's
OCT; Glide and Master). `K.MOD_TARGET_PARAMS` / `generated/ModTargets.h` hold their parameter indices (destination
`62 + k`). Each block `moveDials` (both engines) puts back last block's moved dials, smooths, then adds each
destination's amount (the newest voice's matrix with the custom LFOs, from the last block; the custom LFOs alone
while no voice sounds) to its dial in the dial's own 0..1 knob range (its curve), clamped, whole numbers rounded.
**Rule (owner's):** every dial added to a page or changed must be targetable: append its id as a `p:` option to
the dest menus (append only, so the numbers hold), and give its element `data-param` so the crosshair finds it.
